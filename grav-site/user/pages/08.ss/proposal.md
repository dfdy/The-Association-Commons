## Kuphila likusasa nyalo

Tepolitiki tekuphila likusasa nyalo tisho kuphila budlelwane netindlela tekuhleleka lesifuna kutibona emmangweni ngendlela lesisebentisana ngayo nyalo. Tindlela tikhombisa tinhloso: kubamba lichaza ngekutitsandzela, kutsatsa tincumo ndzawonye nekuphendvulana ngetento tetfu.

I-The Democratic Development Commons iniketa luhlaka lwekutihlela ngekutitsandzela bantfu labangalusebentisa nyalo, ngekufundza ndzawonye, kusitana, nekutibusa ngentsandvo yelinyenti. Emalunga angacoca, alungise, futsi atfutfukise timiso talo emitsetfwenisisekelo yawo nasekutihleleni kwemalanga onkhe, atsatse imitsetfosisekelo yawo njengetivumelwano letiphilako letenta kubamba lichaza kwenteke futsi tigucuke ngekufundza kuloko labahlangabetana nako ndzawonye. Lithulusi lelihlelelwako lekwakha imitsetfosisekelo nelicoco letindlela tekuphatsa lelisatfutfukiswa kuhloswe ngako kusekela lenchubo, emalunga achubeke abe neligunya emitsetfweni layemukelako. Loluhlaka alusiso siphakamiso semtsetfo, futsi kulamukela akutsatsi indzawo yemitsetfo lekhona.

## Kuhlangana kucala ngebantfu.

Bantfu kufanele bakhululeke kuhlangana eceleni kwetidzingo, tinjongo, kanye nematfuba lahlanganyelwe, kanye nekulawula tikhungo labatidalako. Kusebentisana kusho kusebenta ndzawonye kulo lonkhe lemehluko; akudzingi kuvumelana noma kungafaki kungavumelani, kuphikisana noma inselele lehlelekile yemandla.

I-ejensi yentsandvo yelinyenti ayiniketwa labazuzako labangasebenti ngetingcweti, tinhlangano, timakethe noma umbuso. Itfutfuka ngemandla ebantfu lahlangene ekuvisisa timo tabo, kwenta tincumo, kunakekelana, kuphatsa tinsita lekwabelwana ngato, kuphikisa kubusa, kufundza ngetento kanye nekubuyeketa emalungiselelo labaphila ngawo futsi basebenta ndzawonye. Tikhungo tahulumende noma kunjalo tigcina tibopho tato tekuniketa emalungelo, timphahla tahulumende kanye netinsita letiphendvulako.

I-Commons Yekutfutfukisa Intsandvo Yelinyenti iniketa luhlaka lolwabelwana ngalo lwekwenta loku. Itsatsa emasiko lahlukahlukene aseNingizimu Afrika etisebenti, tesive, tekusitana, temfundvo, tekukholwa, tebafundzi, temakhelwane kanye naletinye tinhlangano tekutitsandzela; ngetindlela letilusito letitfutfukiswe kuletinye tindzawo; kanye nekutibophelela kwemtsetfosisekelo enkhululekweni, kulingana, sitfunti, kanye nekuhlanganyela.

Akubeki lifomu linye lenhlangano noma indlela yekutfutfuka. Tinhlangano letehlukene tingagcina tinjongo tato, emagama, emasiko, kanye netakhiwo ngesikhatsi tibuta imibuto levamile yentsandvo yelinyenti mayelana nemandla, kuhlanganyela, lwati, tinsita, kutiphendvulela kanye nelushintjo.

Tinhlangano tihlala tinemsebenti wekubona lifomu letemtsetfo kanye nemisebenti lekhetsekile yemsebenti lehambisana nemsebenti wato.

## Timiso letilishumi tekuhlela.

Letimiso tekuhlela tivulekile kutsi kucocwe ngato, tihlolwe futsi tilungiswe. Baniketa imibuto leyabelwana ngayo yetinhlobo letehlukene tekuhlangana, kunemodeli yinye yenhlangano.

[subsection number="01"]

### Inkhululeko kanye nekuhlangana ngekutitsandzela

Bantfu bangakha, bajoyine, basekele, noma bashiye tinhlangano ngaphandle kwekulawula lokungadzingeki kwangaphandle. Lokulindzelwe kuba lilunga, imisebenti kanye nemiphumela kufanele kuvisiseke ngaphambi kwekutsi bantfu batibophelele.

[/subsection]

[subsection number="02"]

### Kuma lokulinganako

Wonkhe lilunga lisondzelwa njengemuntfu lokhona kucabanga, kwehlulela kanye nekuhlanganyela. Umehluko elwatini, emakhono, lwati, simo, noma umsebenti akudali emazinga laphakeme nalaphansi emalunga.

[/subsection]

[subsection number="03"]

### Kutibusa ngekuhlanganyela

Emalunga agcina ligunya lekugcina ngenjongo yenhlangano, umtsetfosisekelo, kanye nemitfombolusito lemikhulu leyabelwana ngayo. Bameleli kanye netingcweti basebentisa emandla laniketwe wona kunekutsi batsatse sikhundla se-ejensi yemalunga.

[/subsection]

[subsection number="04"]

### Umehluko ngaphandle kwekubusa

Intsandvo yelinyenti ayidzingi kufana. Umehluko kufanele ukhulise emandla lahlanganisiwe kunekutsi ucinise ube budlelwane bekuyala, kuncika, kukhishwa noma kuba ngetulu.

[/subsection]

[subsection number="05"]

### Kuhlanganyela lokubalulekile kanye nekufinyeleleka

Lilungelo lelisemtsetfweni lekuhlanganyela alikeneli lapho khona lulwimi, kufundza nekubhala, kukhubateka, kutfutsa, tindleko, kunakekelwa, umsebenti, kuphepha, tebuchwepheshe, noma simo setenhlalakahle kwenta kutsi kuhlanganyela kungasebenti. Inhlangano yentsandvo yelinyenti kufanele isuse titfombo ngekuklanywa kwemhlaba wonkhe, indzawo yekuhlala lenengcondvo, kanye netindlela letinyenti tekuhlanganyela.

[/subsection]

[subsection number="06"]

### Kufundza ngekuhlanganyela nekubuta

Kuhleleka kuyafundzisa. Emalunga afundza ngemihlangano, imikhankhaso, lucwaningo, kungcubutana, kusabelomali, kunakekela, umsebenti losebentako, kanye nekucabangisisa. Buchwepheshe kufanele kutsi bukhulise kuvisisa lokuhlangene kunekutsi kusungulwe bukhosi lobuphetse lwati.

[/subsection]

[subsection number="07"]

### Kuphatfwa ngekwabelana, kusitana, kanye nemtfwalo wemvelo

Imali, lwati, tikhala, imishini, sikhatsi, kunakekelwa, kanye naletinye tintfo letihlanganyelwe kufanele tisebente tinjongo letivunyelwene ngato kunekucebisa ngasese. Tinhlangano kumele tihlele kusekelana lapho kufanele khona futsi ticabangele imiphumela yemvelo yemisebenti yato. Kusita ngekuvumelana kungenta kutsi kuhlanganyela nekubambisana kube khona ngekwemphahla, kepha akukafaneli kutsi kufiphele kungalingani lokubanti, kutsatse sikhundla sekwabiwa kabusha noma kukhulule tikhungo tahulumende etibopheni letisemtsetfweni.

[/subsection]

[subsection number="08"]

### Emandla ekutiphendvulela, kuphikisa, kanye nekubuyeketa

Ligunya leliniketwe kumele lihlale libonakala, lilinganiselwe, lichazeka, futsi libuyeketwe. Emalunga adzinga tindlela letisebentako tekubuta tincumo, kuveta tikhundla tebantfu labancane, kuphikisa baholi kanye nekufuna kuhlolwa kwekutimela ngaphandle kwekutiphindzisela.

[/subsection]

[subsection number="09"]

### Kutimela kanye nekuhlangana

Tinhlangano kufanele tilawule tinjongo tato ngesikhatsi tihlala tikhululekile kubambisana. Tinhlaka letinkhulu kufanele titfole ligunya etinhlanganweni letikhetsiwe ngekutsi titfunyelwe ngalokucacile kunekutsi tibutsele ndzawonye emandla langadzingeki esikhungwini.

[/subsection]

[subsection number="10"]

### Intfutfuko levulekile nalecabangako

Kute liladi lemhlaba wonkhe kusuka enhlanganweni "yendzabuko" kuya "yesimanje". Tinhlangano kumele tihlole kutsi yini imikhuba yato leyenta kutsi kube khona, kutsi tikhonta tintfo letitsandvwa ngubani, ngukuphi kucabanga lokutsatfwe njengalokubalulekile nekutsi ngukuphi lokuhlangenwe nako lokukhombisa kutsi kufanele kushintje.

[/subsection]

[section-layout label="03 / Luhlaka" surface="shaded"]

## Luhlaka lwekuhlela.

Letibopho tihumusha timiso tibe ngumtsetfosisekelo kanye nemikhuba yemalanga onkhe emalunga langayivisisa, ayisebentise, futsi ayibuyekete ndzawonye.

[subsection number="01"]

### Cala ngenjongo lefanako

Vumelana kutsi yini lehlanganisa bantfu, ngubani lotsintsekako ngumsebenti, yini emalunga lanelitsemba lekuyifeza, ngutiphi timo lafuna kutishintja nekutsi injongo ingakhula njani.

[/subsection]

[subsection number="02"]

### Yenta umtsetfosisekelo ube sivumelwano sekusebenta .

Beka bulunga, kwenta tincumo, emandla laniketwe wona, tinsita, kutiphendvulela, kungcubutana, kuchibiyela, kanye nekuvala ngelulwimi nangemafomethi emalunga langawasebentisa. Umtsetfosisekelo kufanele ubuyeketwe kunekutsi utsatfwe njengembhalo wekusungulwa lonyamalala ube lifayela. [Lithuluzi Lekubhala Umtsetfosisekelo weNhlangano](/ss/stadgar) lelihleliwe litawuhlose kuniketa luhlaka lwekucala lwemalunga kutsi acoce ngalo, aluvumelanise, futsi alutsatse; akukatfolakali. [Umtapolwati wephethini yekuphatsa](/en/governance-patterns) lotfutfukako uniketa tindlela letingakahlolwa teluhlaka lwelucwaningo kanye nekubuyeketwa lokuhlangene.

[/subsection]

[subsection number="03"]

### Gcina tincumo letisisekelo nemalunga .

Hlola kutsi ngutiphi tincumo letidzinga bulunga ngokwalo nekutsi ngutiphi letingadluliselwa. Tingucuko letibalulekile tenjongo, imitsetfo yemtsetfosisekelo, emandla ebuholi noma timphahla letinkhulu letabelwana ngato kufanele kutsi tibuyele emalungwini.

[/subsection]

[subsection number="04"]

### Titfunywa ngaphandle kwekutsi kube nesikhundla sepolitiki

Niketa bameleli ligunya lelanele lekwenta umsebenti lodzingekako, kepha chaza umsebenti wabo, sikhatsi, imisebenti yekubika, kubuyeketa, kanye nekususwa. Kudluliselwa kwemandla kufanele kulandzele budlelwane lobuphindzaphindvwako:

**ligunya → sento → umbiko → kuhlola → kubuyeketa**

[/subsection]

[subsection number="05"]

### Yenta kuhlanganyela kusebente

Yakha tatiso, imihlangano, imibhalo, kuvota, kanye nekuchumana lokutungelete bantfu lokufanele batisebentise. Niketa kufinyelela elulwimini lolufanele, emafomethi lafinyelelekako, kuhlanganyela lokusekelwako kanye nendzawo yekuhlala lefanele ngaphandle kwekuveta umuntfu sicu noma kwetekwelashwa lokungadzingeki.

[/subsection]

[subsection number="06"]

### Sabalalisa lwati kanye nemandla enhlangano

Bhala umsebenti lobalulekile, wabelane ngemakhono bese uhlela kulandzelana. Timali, tebucwepheshe, kwenta lula, budlelwane bangaphandle, kanye nenkumbulo yenhlangano akukafaneli kube yintfo lengakadzingeki kubantfu labadzingekako.

[/subsection]

[subsection number="07"]

### Vikela kuhlangana kwentsandvo yelinyenti kanye nekungavumelani

Dala ematfuba lasimeme ebantfu labasetindzaweni letehlukene tetenhlalakahle kutsi basebentisane ngetinjongo letifanako, ngaphandle kwekwenta shengatsi kungalingani kwesakhiwo sekunyamalele. Phatsa kungavumelani kanye nekucabanga kwebantfu labancane njengemitfombo yelwati kunebufakazi bekungatsembeki.

[/subsection]

[subsection number="08"]

### Steward wabelane ngetinsita kanye nemkhuba wekusitana

Vumelana kutsi imali, imphahla, lwati, kunakekelwa, tikhala, kanye naletinye tintfo letivamile tinikelwa njani, tisetjentiswa njani, tibhalwe phansi futsi tidluliselwa njani. Hlukanisa bumatima ekutiphatseni kabi futsi ugweme kuthulisa kukhipha bantfu ngoba abakhoni kuhlangabetana nendlela lecatjangwako. Lapho khona tidzingo tivela etimeni letibanti tesakhiwo, buta kutsi ngabe imphendvulo lefanele kusitana, kusabalalisa kabusha, simangalo semmango, noma inhlanganisela letsite yaletintfo.

[/subsection]

[subsection number="09"]

### Sungula kuhlola lokutimele

Bantfu labasebentisa ligunya leliniketwe labanye akukafaneli kutsi babe nesibopho sekutibuyeketa bona kuphela. Emalunga kufanele akhetse babuyeketi labatimele labafanele sikali kanye nebungoti benhlangano.

[/subsection]

[subsection number="10"]

### Gcina emarekhodi latsembekile nalafinyelelekako

Rekhoda tincumo, imisebenti kanye nelwati lwetimali emafomini emalunga langawahlola. Lapho kufanele khona, emalunga kufanele acinisekise kutsi emarekhodi emhlangano akhombisa kahle loko lokuncunywe ngako.

[/subsection]

[subsection number="11"]

### Lungisa kungcubutana nekulimala ngendlela lefanele

Hlukanisa kungavumelani nekutiphatsa lokulimatako. Niketa tindlela letifinyelelekako tekuphakamisa kukhatsateka, letinye tindlela uma bantfu labanemagunya batsintseka, ematfuba ekuphendvula, emakhambi lalinganako kanye nekubuyeketa.

[/subsection]

[subsection number="12"]

### Vikela kutimela kwenhlangano

Yenta timo letinkhulu tekukhokha imali, kubambisana, budlelwane betepolitiki kanye nekuncika etikhungweni kubonakale. Emalunga kumele akwati kwehlulela kutsi ngabe kusekelwa kwangaphandle kucondzisa kabusha tintfo letibalulekile, inkhulumo noma kubusa.

[/subsection]

[subsection number="13"]

### Fundza ngetento nangekubuyeketa ngetikhatsi letitsite

Sebentisa sento lesihlangene, imiphumela yaso kanye nekucabangisisa njengemitfombo yelwati. Hlola ngetikhatsi letitsite emandla, budlelwane bentsandvo yelunyenti, imibono kanye nekucabanga lokutsatfwako, luhla lwekutalana kanye nekungafundzi, kuhlanganyela kanye nekufinyelela, bese kubuyiselwa loko lokufundziwe ekwenteni.

[/subsection]

[subsection number="14"]

### Federate ngaphandle kwekuhlanganiswa lokungadzingeki

Lapho khona kubambisana ngesikali lesikhulu kulusito, cacisa kutsi ngumaphi emandla lahlala endzaweni futsi ngumaphi ladluliselwa etulu. Titfunywa kufanele tiphatse emagunya futsi tibike.

[/subsection]

[subsection number="15"]

### Sebentisa umtsetfo njengephansi kanye nemtfombolusito

Hlola ifomu lesemtsetfweni kanye nemitsetfo lekhetsekile yemsebenti lehambisana nenhlangano. Hlangabetana nemalungelo lafanele, kubhaliswa, kubika, kulingana, kungasese, umtselo, kusebenta, tetimali noma leminye imisebenti ngaphandle kwekudida kuhambisana nemtsetfo nelizinga lentsandvo yelinyenti. I [matrix yetemtsetfo](/en/legal-matrix) ngumtfombolusito welucwaningo wesikhashana; emarekhodi ayo ekucala alindzele kucinisekiswa.

[/subsection]

[section-layout label="04 / Ngekwenta"]

## Kubeka kutibusa emsebentini.

Tinhlangano letehlukene tingasebentisa emagama lehlukene, emahhovisi, kanye nemafomu emhlangano. Khetsa emalungiselelo lalingana nemsebenti, ngesikhatsi ugcina ligunya litiphendvulela kumalunga.

[subsection number="01"]

### Emalunga

Emalunga akha sisekelo sentsandvo yelinyenti senhlangano. Emalungelo abo, imisebenti, kwemukelwa, kumiswa, kuphuma, kanye netindlela tekuhlanganyela kufanele kucace.

[/subsection]

[subsection number="02"]

### Umhlangano jikelele noma umhlangano

Inkundla lenkhulu yekutsatsa tincumo yebulungu kufanele kutsi ibhekane netindzaba letibekelwe emalunga, itfole imibiko, ibuyekete ligunya lelidluliselwe futsi ichibiyele imitsetfo lebusako. Ligama lelicondzile, imvamisa kanye nenchubo kungahluka.

[/subsection]

[subsection number="03"]

### Ibhodi, likomidi noma labaphatsi bemahhovisi labatfunyelwe

Ibhodi noma umtimba lolinganako wenta umsebenti emkhatsini wemihlangano yemalunga ngaphakatsi kwemandla lachaziwe. Kuba khona kwayo akukafaneli kuguculele umsebenti wekuphatsa lodluliselwe ube bukhosi ngetulu kwenhlangano.

[/subsection]

[subsection number="04"]

### Emacembu lasebentako kanye nebachubi

Tinhlangano tingakha emacembu esikhashana noma lachubekako emisebentini letsite. Tigunya tabo, tinsita, kanye nebudlelwane bekubika kufanele kutsi bucace, futsi kwenta lula kufanele kutsi kusekele kuhlanganyela kunekutsi kusungulwe ligunya lebuhlakani.

[/subsection]

[subsection number="05"]

### Babuyeketi labatimele

Emalunga akhetsa bantfu labatimele ngalokwenele kulabo labaphetse lenhlangano kutsi babuyekete timali, emagunya, inchubo, kungcubutana kwetinshisekelo kanye naletinye tibopho letibonakalako. Tinhlangano letinkhulu noma letilawulwako tingadzinga kucwaninga ngebungcweti ngetulu kwekubuyeketwa kwentsandvo yelunyenti.

[/subsection]

[subsection number="06"]

### Umtsetfosisekelo, emarekhodi kanye nenkumbulo yenhlangano

Emalunga kumele akwati kutfola imitsetfo lebusako, tincumo letibalulekile, lwati lwetimali, kanye nemarekhodi ladzingekako kute avisise lenhlangano. Inkumbulo yenhlangano kufanele isindze tingucuko tebuholi.

[/subsection]

[subsection number="07"]

### Kucala inhlangano

Luhla lwekuhlola lwekucala lwekucoca nekuvumelanisa ndzawonye:

1. Khomba bantfu, injongo lehlanganyelwe kanye nebantfu labatsintseka ngekwemphahla ngumsebenti.
2. Coca ngebulungu, kuhlanganyela, lulwimi, kufinyelela kwebantfu labakhubatekile kanye netihibe tetintfo letibonakalako ngaphambi kwekulungisa tinchubo.
3. Hlola lifomu lesemtsetfweni kanye nemisebenti lekhetsekile yemsebenti lengasebenta.
4. Vumelana kutsi ngutiphi tincumo letisele kumalunga nekutsi ngutiphi letingadluliselwa.
5. Luhlaka futsi lwamukele ngekuhlanganyela umtsetfosisekelo losebentako.
6. Khetsa bameleli kanye nebabuyeketi labatimele, chaza tigunya tabo bese uhlela kulandzelana.
7. Vumelana kutsi imali, tintfo, emarekhodi, imfihlo, kanye nekungcubutana kwetinshisekelo kutawuphatfwa njani.
8. Rekhoda futsi ucinisekise tincumo tekusungulwa kanye nekubekwa.
9. Hlela emabhange, kubhalisa, kuvunywa kwemtselo noma timvume letilawulwako lapho kudzingeka khona.
10. Cala umsebenti, bese ubuyeketa kutsi imitsetfo ikhicita ini ekusebentiseni bese uyayichibela uma kudzingeka.

[/subsection]

[subsection number="08"]

### Kubuyeketwa lokusihlanu lokuphindzaphindvwako kwentsandvo yelinyenti

Tinhlangano tingasebentisa ngetikhatsi letitsite tindlela tekubuyeketa letisihlanu letimfisha:

**Kuhleleka kanye nemandla:** Emandla agcila kuphi, futsi ngumaphi emakhono lamasha noma ematfuba lavelako?

**Budlelwane bentsandvo yelinyenti:** Ngubani lohlanganyelako, ngubani longaphikisana, emacembu lehlukene ahlobana njani, futsi kuvumelana kufihla kungalingani yini?

**Ideology and doxa:** Yini lenhlangano leyivuzako, leyenta ibe yintfo levamile noma leyenta kubonakale kungenteki, kufaka ekhatsi kucabanga kute umuntfu locabange kuyibuta?

**Luhlu lwekutalana nekungafundzi:** Tigaba tetfu kanye nemikhuba yetfu yavelaphi, futsi siyakudida yini kusebenta kwebungcweti, sikali noma lisiko lelizuzwe njengelifa nesidzingo noma inchubekelembili?

**Kuhlanganyela nekufinyelela:** Ngubani longeke akhone kusebentisa tinchubo letivamile, ngutiphi tihibe letikhicita loko kungafakwa, futsi ngutiphi tingucuko letidzingekako?

Loku kubuyeketwa kufanele kuholele etincumelweni, emisebentini leyabelwe kanye, lapho kufanele khona, kwenta lokuhlangene ngale kwenhlangano kunekutsi kube yimisebenti yekucabangisisa lefanekisako.

[/subsection]

[section-layout label="05 / Luhla lwemagama"]

## Emagama ekuhlela ndzawonye.

Tinshokutsi telulwimi lolulula letisetjentiswa ku-DDC. Leti akusito tintfo letifaka esikhundleni setincazelo temtsetfo tebuchwepheshe lapho khona ligama linenchazelo letsite yemtsetfo.

[glossary]

[glossary-entry]

### Kutiphendvulela

Umsebenti wekuchaza tincumo, kuphendvula labo labatsintsekako, kwemukela kuhlolwa kanye nekulungisa kwehluleka lapho kudzingeka khona.

[/glossary-entry]

[glossary-entry]

### Inhlangano

Bantfu labahlela ngekutitsandzela bazungeze injongo lefanako futsi bavumelane kutsi batawubusa njani umsebenti wabo lohlangene.

[/glossary-entry]

[glossary-entry]

### Kusebenta kahle ngekuhlanganyela

Inkholelo yelicembu lelinesipiliyoni, lesekelwe ekusebentiseni, kutsi lingasebenta ndzawonye futsi litsintse timo talo.

[/glossary-entry]

[glossary-entry]

### Kusebentisana

Bantfu labahlanganisa lwati lolwehlukene, emakhono, kanye nemitfombolusito ekwenteni ngekwabelana ngesikhatsi bagcina likhono lekungavumelani, kuphikisana nemandla kanye nekuphikisa timo labasebenta ngato ndzawonye.

[/glossary-entry]

[glossary-entry]

### Tintfo letivamile

Tinsita, lwati, sakhiwonchanti noma emakhono laphethwe noma labuswa ngenhloso lehlanganyelwe ngaphasi kwemitsetfo leyentiwe noma lemukelwe bantfu labatsintsekako.

[/glossary-entry]

[glossary-entry]

### Kuphelelisa

Budlelwane lapho khona umehluko ufaka sandla emandleni lahlanganyelwe ngaphandle kwekubekwa etikhundleni letiphakeme naletiphansi.

[/glossary-entry]

[glossary-entry]

### Umtsetfosisekelo

Imitsetfo lebusako levunyelwe yinhlangano lephatselene nenjongo, kuba lilunga, ligunya, kwenta tincumo, tinsita, kutiphendvulela, kuchibiyela, kanye nekuvalwa.

[/glossary-entry]

[glossary-entry]

### Kutfumela

Kunika umuntfu noma umtimba ligunya lelilinganiselwe lekusebenta egameni lenhlangano ngaphandle kwekudlulisela ligunya lekugcina lebulungu.

[/glossary-entry]

[glossary-entry]

### Intfutfuko yentsandvo yelinyenti

Kutfutfukiswa lokuvulekile kwetimo kanye nemandla lahlanganisiwe lapho khona bantfu bangavisisa, babuse, futsi bagucule imphilo yabo lefanako, ngaphandle kwekutsatsa indlela yemhlaba wonkhe yenchubekelembili.

[/glossary-entry]

[glossary-entry]

### Kuphikisana

Kuveta kungavumelani nesincumo lesiphakanyisiwe noma lesikhona, inchazelo noma umkhuba. Ku-DDC kutsatfwa njengemtfombo longaba khona welwati kunekungatsembeki.

[/glossary-entry]

[glossary-entry]

### Kubusa

Budlelwane lobuhlala sikhatsi lesidze lapho khona labanye bantfu noma tikhungo tisebentisa emandla langaphendvuli noma laphakeme ngekwesakhiwo kulabanye.

[/glossary-entry]

[glossary-entry]

### Doxa

Tintfo leticatjangwako kanye nemikhuba lebonakala iyimvelo noma isobala kangangekutsi ivame kukhicitwa kabusha ngaphandle kwekutsi kuphikiswane ngayo ngekucaphela.

[/glossary-entry]

[glossary-entry]

### Inhlangano

Kusebentisana emkhatsini wetinhlangano letitimele ngekusebentisa umtimba lowabelwana ngawo emandla awo aniketwa ngalokucacile tinhlangano letiyincenye yawo.

[/glossary-entry]

[glossary-entry]

### Inkhululeko yekuhlangana

Inkhululeko yekwakha, kujoyina, kusekela, noma kushiya tinhlangano kanye nekusebenta ngekuhlanganyela nalabanye, ngekuya ngemalungelo emtsetfosisekelo kanye nemtsetfo losebentako.

[/glossary-entry]

[glossary-entry]

### Kulandzelana kwetigaba

Buhlobo lobuhleliwe bekuba ngetulu kanye nekuba ngaphansi. Umehluko endzimeni noma bungcweti akusiko kulandzelana ngekuzenzakalela; indaba kutsi emandla akhiwe njani.

[/glossary-entry]

[glossary-entry]

### Imibono yekusebenta

Tindlela tikhungo kanye netindlela tekusebenta letichubeka tikhulumisana ngaloko lokukhona, lokubalulekile, lokungenteka nekutsi ngutiphi tinhlobo tetindzima tetenhlalo bantfu labalindzeleke kutsi batitsatse.

[/glossary-entry]

[glossary-entry]

### Kubuyeketwa lokutimele

Kuhlolwa lokwentiwa bantfu labatimele ngalokwenele kulabo labahlolwa tincumo tabo, timali noma kuphatsa kwabo.

[/glossary-entry]

[glossary-entry]

### Ligunya

Injongo, ligunya kanye nemikhawulo leniketwa ummeleli, sitfunywa, likomidi noma umphatsi wesikhundla.

[/glossary-entry]

[glossary-entry]

### Bukhosi belilunga

Umtsetfo wekutsi ligunya lekugcina kulenhlangano lisemalungwini ayo kunebhodi yayo, tisebenti, baxhasi noma tingcweti tangephandle.

[/glossary-entry]

[glossary-entry]

### Kusitana

Inhlangano levumelanako lapho khona bantfu bahlanganisa emakhono noma tinsita kute bahlangabetane netidzingo letihlanganyelwe, kunekupha lokucondziswe ngendlela yinye kusuka kubasiti kuya kubazuzi.

[/glossary-entry]

[glossary-entry]

### Kufanekisa

Kuprakthiza etikhungweni letikhona nyalo budlelwane bentsandvo yelinyenti kanye netinhlobo tekubambisana bantfu labafuna kwenta kutsi kube khona kakhulu.

[/glossary-entry]

[glossary-entry]

### Indlela yekusebenta

Budlelwane lobuphindzaphindvwako emkhatsini wesento nekucabangisisa lapho khona bantfu bahumusha timo, benta, bafundze emiphumeleni futsi babuyeketa loko labakwenta.

[/glossary-entry]

[glossary-entry]

### Indzawo yekuhlala lefanele

Kulungiswa lokudzingekako nalokufanele lokwenta umuntfu lokhubatekile ahlanganyele ngekulingana lapho khona tinyatselo tekufinyeleleka jikelele tingakeneli, ngaphandle kwekubeka umtfwalo longalinganiko.

[/glossary-entry]

[glossary-entry]

### Kusabalalisa kabusha

Lushintjo lwemabomu lwekutsi tintfo letibonakalako, ematfuba, tinzuzo noma imitfwalo isabalaliswa njani, kungaba ngaphakatsi kwenhlangano noma ngemalungiselelo lamakhulu etenhlalo nemmango.

[/glossary-entry]

[glossary-entry]

### Injongo lefanako

Inhloso lefanako lenika inhlangano sizatfu sekuba khona futsi icondzise kusetjentiswa kwayo kwetinsita.

[/glossary-entry]

[glossary-entry]

### Kuhlanganyela lokusekelwako

Kuhlanganyela lapho khona umuntfu asebentisa lusito, kuhumusha, kusekela tekuchumana, noma lolunye lusito ngesikhatsi agcina i-ejensi yakhe kanye nesimo sakhe sepolitiki.

[/glossary-entry]

[glossary-entry]

### Umklamo wemhlaba wonkhe

Kuklama tindzawo, lwati, tinchubo kanye nemathuluzi kute asetjentiswe bantfu labanyenti labasebentako kusukela ekucaleni kunekutsi batsembele kuphela ekulungiseni ngamunye kamuva.

[/glossary-entry]

[glossary-entry]

### Inhlangano yekutitsandzela

Inhlangano leyakhiwa ngekuvumelana emkhatsini webantfu kunekutsi kube lilunga leliphocelelekile noma kudalwa ngaphambilini ngumbuso.

[/glossary-entry]

[/glossary]
