## Kuphila likusasa nyalo

Tepolitiki tekuphila likusasa nyalo tisho kuphila budlelwane netindlela tekuhleleka lesifuna kutibona emmangweni ngendlela lesisebentisana ngayo nyalo. Tindlela tikhombisa tinhloso: kubamba lichaza ngekutitsandzela, kutsatsa tincumo ndzawonye nekuphendvulana ngetento tetfu.

Loluhlaka lunikezwa njengesibonelo lesingasetjentiswa nyalo: bantfu bangakhetsa kusebentisa, kugucula nekutfutfukisa timiso talo kuma-stadgar abo (imitsetfosisekelo noma imitsetfo yenhlangano) nasekuhleleni kwemalanga onkhe, ngaphambi kwekwamukelwa kwaloluhlaka loluphakanyisiwe ngumtsetfo waseNingizimu Afrika esikhatsini lesitako. Emandla alo njengemkhuba lohlanganyelwako avela kubantfu labalamukelako. Kulamukela akulwenti luhlaka lube ngumtsetfo futsi akutsatsi indzawo yemtsetfo lokhona.

[/remember-panel]

# siSwati

Umtsetfo lohlongozwako awukahunyushelwa kulolulwimi. Lena yiphrojekthi yelulwimi letimele.

[/hero-intro]

Iphrojekthi ngayinye yelulwimi ilawulwa yi-stadgar yayo (umtsetfosisekelo noma imitsetfo yenhlangano). Emalunga ancuma tinhloso, kutsatsa tincumo nebulunga ngekwaleyo mitsetfo. Kukhombisa inshisekelo lapha akukwenti lilunga ngekutentakalela.

[section-layout label="06 / Participate"]

## Khombisa inshisekelo yekujoyina lephrojekthi

Sicelo sekubamba lichaza kulephrojekthi yelulwimi. Lelifomu likhombisa inshisekelo kuphela; iphrojekthi ibeka bulunga nge-stadgar yayo.

[/section-layout]
