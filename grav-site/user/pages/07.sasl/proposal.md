## Prefiguration

Prefigurative politics means practising the relationships and ways of organising we want to see in society through how we act together now. Here, the means reflect the ends: voluntary participation, shared decision-making and accountability to one another.

This draft is offered as a de facto template: people can choose to use, adapt and develop its principles in their own stadgar and everyday organising before any future legislative recognition of this proposed framework in South Africa. Its authority as a shared practice comes from the people who adopt it. Adoption does not enact this draft or replace existing law.

[/remember-panel]
