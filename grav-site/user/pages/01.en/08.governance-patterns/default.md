# Governance-pattern library

A public library of constitutional options for the [Association Constitution Drafting Tool](/en/stadgar). Rather than prescribing one template, it helps members compare ways to organise, understand their consequences, and adapt rules together.

**Initial research release — untested draft patterns.** The aim is to build a library of tested patterns through participatory use, review, and revision. These initial entries have not yet been field-tested or legally reviewed. None is ready for automatic use by the generator.

Each pattern explains the problem it addresses, when it may help, when it may be unsuitable, choices for members, and risks and safeguards. Illustrative clauses contain placeholders; they are starting points for discussion, not ready-to-adopt constitutions or statements of legal compliance.

## Learning from use

Testing status and legal review are separate. A pattern should only be labelled **tested** when its record includes evidence of use, context, findings, limitations, a testing date, and a reviewer. Testing in one association does not establish suitability elsewhere. Generator readiness also requires recorded legal review and a review schedule, followed by checks against the association’s context and current law.

The library supports constitutions as living documents that enable and enhance collective participation. Related entries in the [legal matrix](/en/legal-matrix) identify research questions, rather than certifying that a pattern complies with the law. This English-only resource develops [the white paper’s governance-pattern library agenda](/en/white-paper#part-i-25).
