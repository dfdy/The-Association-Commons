# Contact

Express your interest in the Democratic Development Commons, ask about participating, or propose a community-led translation project. You can use the message field to name your language or variety, describe the project you have in mind, and explain how you would like to contribute or take part in its collective direction.

We welcome proposals for languages and varieties that are not listed in the language menu or recognised by the language-suggestion form. Speakers and communities should help shape their own translations, terminology, and ways of working together.

Submitting this form expresses interest; it does not automatically confer membership, establish a translation project, or subscribe you to a mailing list. Please avoid sensitive personal information and read the [privacy policy](/en/privacy).
