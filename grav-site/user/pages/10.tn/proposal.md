## Go tshela bokamoso gone jaanong

Dipolotiki tsa go tshela bokamoso gone jaanong di raya go diragatsa dikamano le mekgwa ya go rulaganya e re batlang go e bona mo setšhabeng ka tsela e re dirang mmogo ka yone gompieno. Mekgwa e bontsha maikaelelo: go tsaya karolo ka boithaopo, go dira ditshwetso mmogo le go ikarabela mo go ba bangwe.

The Democratic Development Commons e neela letlhomeso la go ithulaganya ka boithaopo le batho ba ka le dirisang gone jaanong, ka go ithuta mmogo, go thusana, le go ipusa ka temokerasi. Maloko a ka buisana ka melawana ya lone, a e fetola, le go e tlhabolola mo melaotheong ya bone le mo go ithulaganyeng ga letsatsi le letsatsi, ba tsaya melaotheo ya bone e le ditumalano tse di tshelang tse di kgontshang go tsaya karolo le go fetoga ka maitemogelo a a abelanwang. Sediriswa se se rulaganyediwang go tlhama melaotheo le laeborari ya mekgwa ya tsamaiso e e tlhabololwang di ikaelela go tshegetsa thulaganyo eno, fa maloko a ntse a boloka taolo mo melaong e ba e amogelang. Letlhomeso leno ga se molao o o tshitshintsweng, mme go le amogela ga go tseye sebaka sa melao e e leng teng.

[/remember-panel]

# Kgololesego ya go [hero-accent]rulaganya.[/hero-accent]

[hero-accent]Go ithuta mmogo, go thusana, le go ipusa ka temokerasi:[/hero-accent] letlhomeso la Aforika Borwa la gore batho ba age mekgatlho, ba tlhokomele metswedi e e abelanwang, le go dirisana mmogo ntle le go neela maatla a bone a go tsaya ditshwetso mmogo.

[/hero-intro]

**Re ithuta temokerasi ka go dira temokerasi.** Se ke letlhomeso la go rulaganya la boithaopo, e seng molao o o tshitshintsweng. Ga e tseye sebaka sa molao wa Aforika Borwa kgotsa go neela botho jwa semolao, kwadiso, kgotsa maemo a lekgetho.


[section-layout label="01 / Kgopolo"]

## Botsalano bo simolola ka batho.

Batho ba tshwanetse go gololesega go kopana go dikologa ditlhokego tse di abelanwang, maikaelelo, le dikgonagalo, le go laola ditheo tse ba di tlhamang. Tirisanommogo e kaya go dira mmogo go kgabaganya dipharologano; ga e tlhoke kutlwano kgotsa go kgaphela kwa thoko go sa dumalane, go ganetsana kgotsa kgwetlho e e rulagantsweng ya puso.

Boemedi jwa temokerasi ga bo abelwe bajaboswa ba ba sa direng sepe ke baitseanape, mekgatlho, mebaraka kgotsa puso. E tlhabologa ka bokgoni jwa batho jo bo kopanetsweng jwa go tlhaloganya maemo a bona, go tsaya ditshwetso, go tlhokomelana, go tlhokomela metswedi e e abelanwang, go gwetlha taolo, go ithuta mo tirong le go boeletsa dithulaganyo tse ka tsona ba tshelang le go dira mmogo. Ditheo tsa setšhaba le fa go ntse jalo di boloka ditlamego tsa tsona tsa go tlamela ka ditshwanelo, dithoto tsa setšhaba le ditirelo tse di nang le maikarabelo.

Dikgolagano tsa Tlhabololo ya Temokerasi di tlamela ka letlhomeso le le abelanwang la go dira seno. E tsaya mo dingwaong tse di farologaneng tsa Aforika Borwa tsa mokgatlho wa badiri, wa baagi, wa thuso-mmogo, wa thuto, wa tumelo, wa baithuti, wa boagisani le wa mokgatlho o mongwe wa boithaopo; ka mekgwa e e mosola e e tlhamilweng go sele; le ka ga maitlamo a molaotheo a kgololesego, tekatekano, seriti, le go tsaya karolo.

Ga e laele sebopego se le sengwe sa mokgatlho kgotsa tsela ya tlhabololo. Mekgatlho e e farologaneng e ka boloka maikaelelo a yona, maina, meetlo, le dipopego fa e ntse e botsa dipotso tse di tlwaelegileng tsa temokerasi ka ga maatla, go tsaya karolo, kitso, didirisiwa, boikarabelo le phetogo.

Mekgatlho e sa ntse e na le maikarabelo a go supa sebopego sa semolao le ditiro tse di rileng tsa tiro tse di maleba le tiro ya bona.

[/section-layout]


[section-layout label="02 / Metheo"]

## Melaometheo e le lesome ya go rulaganya.

Melaometheo eno ya go rulaganya e ka tlotliwa ka yone, ya lekwa le go tokafadiwa. Di fana ka dipotso tse arolelanoeng bakeng sa mefuta e fapaneng ya mokgatlo, ho ena le mohlala o le mong wa mokgatlo.

[subsection number="01"]

### Kgololesego le mokgatlho wa boithaopo

Batho ba ka nna ba bopa, ba tsenela, ba tshegetsa, kgotsa ba tlogela mekgatlho kwantle ga taolo e e sa tlhokegeng ya kwa ntle. Ditebelelo tsa botokololo, maikarabelo le ditlamorago di tshwanetse go tlhaloganyesega pele batho ba dira.

[/subsection]

[subsection number="02"]

### Boemo jo bo lekanang

Leloko lengwe le lengwe le atamelwa jaaka motho yo o kgonang go akanya, go atlhola le go tsaya karolo. Dipharologano tsa kitso, bokgoni, maitemogelo, maemo, kgotsa maikarabelo ga di tlhole ditlhopha tse di kwa godimo le tse di kwa tlase tsa maloko.

[/subsection]

[subsection number="03"]

### Boipuso jwa kopanelo

Maloko a na le taolo e e kwa godimo mo boikaelelong jwa mokgatlho, molaotheo, le didirisiwa tse dikgolo tse di abelanwang. Baemedi le baitseanape ba dirisa dithata tse di abetsweng go na le go emisetsa boemedi jwa maloko.

[/subsection]

[subsection number="04"]

### Pharologano e e senang taolo

Temokerasi ga e tlhoke go tshwana. Dipharologano di tshwanetse go godisa bokgoni jo bo kopanetsweng go na le go thatafatsa go nna dikamano tsa taelo, go ikaega, go kgaphelwa thoko kgotsa bogolo.

[/subsection]

[subsection number="05"]

### Botsayakarolo jo bo tseneletseng le phitlhelelo

Tshwanelo ya semmuso ya go tsaya karolo ga e a lekana fa puo, kitso ya go bala le go kwala, bogole, dipalangwa, ditshenyegelo, tlhokomelo, tiro, pabalesego, thekenoloji, kgotsa maemo a loago di dira gore go tsaya karolo go se ka ga dirisiwa. Mokgatlho wa temokerasi o tshwanetse go tlosa dikgoreletsi ka go dira polane ya lefatshe lotlhe, bonno jo bo utlwalang, le ditsela tse dintsi tsa go tsaya karolo.

[/subsection]

[subsection number="06"]

### Go ithuta mmogo le go batlisisa

Thulaganyo e a ruta. Maloko a ithuta ka dikopano, matsholo, dipatlisiso, kgotlhang, go dira tekanyetsokabo, tlhokomelo, tiro e e mosola, le go akanya. Boitseanape bo tshwanetse go godisa kutlwisiso e e kopanetsweng go na le go tlhoma taolo e le nosi mo kitsong.

[/subsection]

[subsection number="07"]

### Botlhokomedi jo bo tlhakanetsweng, go thusana, le boikarabelo jwa ikholoji

Madi, kitso, mafelo, didirisiwa, nako, tlhokomelo, le dilo tse dingwe tse di abelanwang di tshwanetse go direla maikaelelo a go dumalanweng ka one go na le go humisa ka namana. Mekgatlho e tshwanetse go rulaganya tshegetso ya go dirisana fa go tshwanelang le go akanyetsa ditlamorago tsa ikholoji tsa ditiro tsa yona. Thuso ya bobedi e ka dira gore go tsaya karolo le go nna seoposengwe go kgonege ka dithoto, fela ga go a tshwanela go fifatsa go tlhoka tekatekano go go anameng, go emisetsa kabosešwa kgotsa go imolola ditheo tsa setšhaba mo ditlamego tse di ka fa molaong.

[/subsection]

[subsection number="08"]

### Maatla a a nang le maikarabelo, go sa dumalane, le tshekatsheko

Taolo e e abetsweng e tshwanetse go nna e bonala, e lekanyeditswe, e tlhalosiwa, e bile e ka sekasekwa. Maloko a tlhoka ditsela tse di mosola tsa go botsa ditshwetso, go tlhagisa maemo a bonnye, go gwetlha baeteledipele le go batla tlhatlhobo e e ikemetseng kwa ntle ga go ipusolosetsa.

[/subsection]

[subsection number="09"]

### Boipuso le kopano

Mekgatlho e tshwanetse go laola maikaelelo a yone fa e ntse e gololesegile go dirisana mmogo. Ditheo tse dikgolo di tshwanetse go bona taolo go tswa mo mekgatlhong ya ditlhopho ka go abela ba bangwe ka tlhamalalo go na le go kokoanya maatla a a sa tlhokegeng kwa setheong.

[/subsection]

[subsection number="10"]

### Tlhabololo e e bulegileng le e e akanyang

Ga go na lere ya lefatshe lotlhe go tswa mo mokgatlhong wa "setso" go ya kwa "segompienong". Mekgatlho e tshwanetse go sekaseka gore mekgwa ya yona e dira eng se se kgonegang, gore e direla dikgatlhego tsa bomang, ke dikakanyo dife tse di setseng di tsewa tsia le gore ke maitemogelo afe a a akantshang gore a tshwanetse go fetoga.

[/subsection]

[/section-layout]


[section-layout label="03 / Letlhomeso" surface="shaded"]

## Letlhomeso la go rulaganya.

Maitlamo ano a fetolela melawana go nna melaotheo le ditiro tsa letsatsi le letsatsi tse maloko a ka di tlhaloganyang, a di dirisang, le go di boeletsa mmogo.

[subsection number="01"]

### Simolola ka maikaelelo a a tlhakanetsweng

Dumelana gore ke eng se se kopanyang batho, ke mang yo o amiwang ke tiro, ke eng se maloko a solofelang go se fitlhelela, ke maemo afe a ba batlang go a fetola le gore maikaelelo a ka tlhagelela jang.

[/subsection]

[subsection number="02"]

### Dira gore molaotheo e nne tumalano e e dirang

Beha botho, ho nka qeto, matla a abetsweng, mehlodi, boikarabelo, kgohlano, phetolo, le ho kwalwa ka puo le difomate tseo ditho di ka di sebedisang. Molaotheo o tshwanetse go tlhabololwa go na le go tsewa jaaka tokomane ya motheo e e nyelelang mo faeleng. [Sediriswa sa go Kwala Molaotheo wa Mokgatlho](/tn/stadgar) se se rulagantsweng se tla ikaelela go tlamela ka terafote ya tshimologo gore maloko a e buisane, a e tlwaele, le go e amogela; ga e ise e nne teng. [Laeborari ya paterone ya puso](/en/governance-patterns) e e tlhabologang e neelana ka dikgetho tsa terafote tse di sa lekiwang tsa patlisiso le tshekatsheko e e kopanetsweng.

[/subsection]

[subsection number="03"]

### Boloka ditshwetso tsa motheo le maloko

Supa gore ke ditshwetso dife tse di tlhokang botokololo ka bojone le gore ke dife tse di ka abetsweng ba bangwe. Diphetogo tse di botlhokwa tsa maikaelelo, melawana ya molaotheo, dithata tsa boeteledipele kgotsa dithoto tse dikgolo tse di abelanwang di tshwanetse go boela kwa malokong.

[/subsection]

[subsection number="04"]

### Romela baemedi kwa ntle ga kemedi ya sepolotiki

Fa baemedi taolo e e lekaneng go dira tiro e e tlhokegang, mme o tlhalose thomo ya bona, nako, ditiro tsa go bega, tshekatsheko, le go tlosiwa. Kabelo e tshwanetse go latela kamano e e ipoeletsang:

**taelo → kgato → pegelo → tlhatlhobo → poeletso**

[/subsection]

[subsection number="05"]

### Dira gore go nna le seabe go nne mosola

Rala dikitsiso, dikopano, ditokomane, go bouta, le puisano go dikologa batho ba ba tshwanetseng go di dirisa. Fana ka phihlello e loketseng ya puo, difomete tse fihlellwang, ho nka karolo ho tshehetswang le bodulo bo utlwahalang ntle le tshenolo e sa hlokeheng ya botho kapa ya bongaka.

[/subsection]

[subsection number="06"]

### Go anamisa kitso le bokgoni jwa go rulaganya

Kwala tiro e e botlhokwa, abelana bokgoni le go rulaganya tatelano. Ditšhelete, thekenoloji, go tlhofofatsa, dikamano tsa kwa ntle, le memori wa mokgatlho ga di a tshwanela go nna tsepamisitswe go sa tlhokege mo bathong ba ba botlhokwa thata.

[/subsection]

[subsection number="07"]

### Sireletsa go kopana ga temokerasi le go sa dumalane

Tlhama ditšhono tse di tswelelang tsa batho go ralala dipharologano tsa loago go dira mmogo ka maikaelelo a a tlhakanetsweng, ntle le go dira jaaka e kete go tlhoka tekatekano ga dipopego go nyeletse. Tsaya go sa dumalane le go ntsha mabaka ga batho ba le mmalwa jaaka metswedi ya tshedimosetso go na le bosupi jwa go sa ikanyege.

[/subsection]

[subsection number="08"]

### Motlhokomedi o ne a abelana didirisiwa le go ikatisa go thusana

Dumalana ka moo madi, dithoto, kitso, tlhokomelo, diphatlha, le didirisiwa tse dingwe tse di tlwaelegileng di ntshiwang, di dirisiwang, di kwalwang le go fetisiwa ka teng. Farologanya mathata le boitshwaro jo bo sa siamang mme o tile go kgaphela batho kwa thoko ka tidimalo ka gonne ba sa kgone go fitlhelela tlwaelo e e tsewang. Moo ditlhokego di tlhagelelang go tswa mo maemong a a anameng a popego, botsa gore a karabo e e maleba ke thuso ya bobedi, kabosešwa, tleleimi ya setšhaba, kgotsa kopanyo nngwe ya tseno.

[/subsection]

[subsection number="09"]

### Tlhoma tlhatlhobo e e ikemetseng

Batho ba ba dirisang taolo e ba e abetsweng ga ba a tshwanela go nna le boikarabelo jwa go itlhatlhoba fela. Maloko a tshwanetse go tlhoma basekaseki ba ba ikemetseng ba ba maleba le selekanyo le dikotsi tsa mokgatlho.

[/subsection]

[subsection number="10"]

### Boloka direkoto tse di ikanyegang le tse di fitlhelesegang

Rekota ditshwetso, maikarabelo le tshedimosetso ya matlole mo diforomong tse maloko a ka di tlhatlhobang. Fa go tshwanetseng, maloko a tshwanetse go netefatsa gore direkoto tsa dikopano di supa sentle se se tserweng tshwetso.

[/subsection]

[subsection number="11"]

### Rarabolola dikgotlhang le dikotsi ka tekatekano

Farologanya go sa dumalane le boitshwaro jo bo kotsi. Tlamela ka ditsela tse di fitlhelesegang tsa go tlhagisa matshwenyego, ditsela tse dingwe fa batho ba ba maatla ba amega, ditšhono tsa go tsiboga, dipheko tse di lekanang le tshekatsheko.

[/subsection]

[subsection number="12"]

### Sireletsa boipuso jwa mokgatlho

Dira gore maemo a magolo a matlole, ditirisanommogo, dikamano tsa sepolotiki le go ikaega ga ditheo di bonale. Maloko a tshwanetse go kgona go atlhola gore a tshegetso ya kwa ntle e kaela gape ditlapele, puo kgotsa puso.

[/subsection]

[subsection number="13"]

### Ithute ka go tsaya kgato le go boeletsa nako le nako

Dirisa tiro ya setlhopha, ditlamorago tsa yona le go akanya jaaka metswedi ya kitso. Nako le nako tlhatlhoba maatla, dikamano tsa temokerasi, kakanyo le dikakanyo tse di tsewang jaaka tse di tlwaelegileng, losika le go sa ithute, go tsaya karolo le phitlhelelo, morago ga moo o busetse se se ithutilweng mo tirong.

[/subsection]

[subsection number="14"]

### Federate kwa ntle ga go kopanya dilo mo lefelong le le rileng go go sa tlhokegeng

Moo tirisanommogo ka selekanyo se segolo e leng mosola, totobatsa gore ke dithata dife tse di salang tsa selegae le gore ke dife tse di abetsweng kwa godimo. Baemedi ba tshwanetse go rwala ditaelo le go bega.

[/subsection]

[subsection number="15"]

### Dirisa molao jaaka motheo le motswedi

Supa sebopego sa semolao le melawana e e rileng ya tiro e e maleba le mokgatlho. Kopana le ditshwanelo tse di maleba, kwadiso, go bega, tekatekano, sephiri, lekgetho, tiro, ditšhelete kgotsa ditiro tse dingwe ntle le go tlhakatlhakanya kobamelo ya semolao le boleng jwa temokerasi. [Matrix ya semolao](/en/legal-matrix) ke motswedi wa patlisiso wa nakwana; direkoto tsa yone tsa ntlha di emetse go netefadiwa.

[/subsection]

[/section-layout]


[section-layout label="04 / Mo Tirong"]

## Go tsenya boipuso mo tirisong.

Mekgatlho e e farologaneng e ka dirisa maina a a farologaneng, diofisi le diforomo tsa dikopano. Tlhopha dithulaganyo tse di lekanang le tiro, fa o ntse o boloka bolaodi bo ikarabela mo malokong.

[subsection number="01"]

### Maloko

Maloko a bopa motheo wa temokerasi wa mokgatlho. Ditshwanelo tsa bone, maikarabelo, kamogelo, go emisiwa, go tswa, le ditsela tsa go tsaya karolo di tshwanetse go tlhaloganyesega sentle.

[/subsection]

[subsection number="02"]

### Kopanokakaretso kgotsa kokoano

Foramo e kgolo ya go tsaya ditshwetso ya maloko e tshwanetse go samagana le merero e e beetsweng maloko, go amogela dipegelo, go sekaseka taolo e e abetsweng le go baakanya melawana e e laolang. Leina le le tlhomameng, makgetlo le tsamaiso di ka farologana.

[/subsection]

[subsection number="03"]

### Boto, komiti kgotsa batlhankedi ba ba abetsweng tiro

Boto kgotsa setheo se se lekanang se dira tiro magareng ga dikopano tsa maloko mo teng ga dithata tse di tlhalositsweng. Go nna teng ga yona ga go a tshwanela go fetola maikarabelo a khuduthamaga a a abetsweng go nna bolaodi mo mokgatlhong.

[/subsection]

[subsection number="04"]

### Ditlhopha tsa tiro le batsamaisi

Mekgatlho e ka tlhama ditlhopha tsa nakwana kgotsa tse di tswelelang tsa ditiro tse di rileng. Ditaelo tsa bona, didiriswa, le dikamano tsa go bega di tshwanetse go tlhaloganyesega, mme go tsamaisa di tshwanetse go tshegetsa go tsaya karolo go na le go tlhoma taolo ya tlhaloganyo.

[/subsection]

[subsection number="05"]

### Batlhatlhobi ba ba ikemetseng

Maloko a tlhoma batho ba ba ikemetseng ka nosi mo go lekaneng mo go ba ba tsamaisang mokgatlho go sekaseka matlole, ditaelo, tsamaiso, dikgotlhang tsa dikgatlhego le ditlamego tse dingwe tsa dilo tse di bonalang. Mekgatlho e megolo kgotsa e e laolwang e ka tlhoka boruni jwa seporofešenale mo godimo ga tshekatsheko ya temokerasi.

[/subsection]

[subsection number="06"]

### Molaotheo, direkoto le mogopolo wa mokgatlho

Maloko a tshwanetse go kgona go bona melao e e laolang, ditshwetso tse di botlhokwa, tshedimosetso ya madi le direkoto tse di tlhokegang go tlhaloganya mokgatlho. Kgopolo ya mokgatlho e tshwanetse go falola diphetogo tsa boeteledipele.

[/subsection]

[subsection number="07"]

### Go simolola mokgatlho

Lenaanenetefatso la tshimologo go buisana le go tlwaela mmogo:

1. Supa batho, maikaelelo a a tlhakanetsweng le batho ba ba amiwang ke tiro ka dilo tse di bonalang.
2. Buisana ka botokololo, go tsaya karolo, puo, phitlhelelo ya bogole le dikgoreletsi tsa didirisiwa pele ga go baakanya ditsamaiso.
3. Supa sebopego sa semolao le ditiro tse di rileng tsa tiro tse di ka nnang tsa dira.
4. Dumalana gore ke ditshwetso dife tse di setseng mo malokong le gore ke dife tse di ka abelwang.
5. Thala le go amogela mmogo molaotheo o o ka dirisiwang.
6. Tlhopha baemedi le basekaseki ba ba ikemetseng, tlhalosa ditaelo tsa bone le go rulaganya tatelano.
7. Dumelana kafa madi, dithoto, direkoto, sephiri le dikgotlhang tsa dikgatlhego di tla rarabololwang ka gone.
8. Rekota le go netefatsa ditshwetso tsa go tlhoma le go thapiwa.
9. Rulaganya banka, ikwadiso, ditumelelo tsa lekgetho kgotsa ditetlelelo tse di laolwang fa go tlhokegang.
10. Simolola tiro, morago ga moo boeletsa se melao e se tlhagisang mo tirisong mme o e baakanye fa go tlhokega.

[/subsection]

[subsection number="08"]

### Ditshekatsheko tse tlhano tse di ipoeletsang tsa temokerasi

Mekgatlho e ka dirisa nako le nako mekgwa e metlhano ya tshekatsheko e khutshwane:

**Tatelano ya maemo le bokgoni:** Maatla a tsepamisiwa kae, mme ke bokgoni bofe jo bošwa kgotsa dikgonagalo tse di tlhagelelang?

**Dikamano tsa temokerasi:** Ke mang yo o tsayang karolo, ke mang yo o ka ganetsanang, ditlhopha tse di farologaneng di amana jang, mme a kutlwano e fitlha go tlhoka tekatekano?

**Ideology le doxa:** Ke eng se mokgatlho o se duelang, o se tlwaetsa kgotsa o se dirang gore se lebege se sa kgonege, go akaretsa le dikakanyo tse go se nang ope yo o akantseng go di botsa?

**Losika le go sa ithute:** Ditlhopha le ditlwaelo tsa rona di tswa kae, mme a re tlhakanya go dira seporofešenale, selekanyo kgotsa ngwao e e ruilweng ka boswa le tlhokego kgotsa kgatelopele?

**Go tsaya karolo le phitlhelelo:** Ke bomang ba ba ka se kgoneng go dirisa dithulaganyo tse di tlwaelegileng, ke dikgoreletsi dife tse di tlhagisang go kgaphelwa thoko goo, mme ke diphetogo dife tse di tlhokegang?

Ditshekatsheko tseno di tshwanetse go lebisa kwa ditshwetsong, maikarabelong a a abetsweng le, fa go tshwanelang, kgato e e kopanetsweng go feta mokgatlho go na le go nna ditiro tsa go akanya tsa tshwantshetso.

[/subsection]

[/section-layout]


[section-layout label="05 / Lenanefoko la Mafoko"]

## Mafoko a go rulaganya mmogo.

Ditlhaloso tsa puo e e tlwaelegileng tse di dirisiwang mo DDC. Tsena ha se sebaka sa ditlhaloso tsa molao tsa sethekniki moo lereo le nang le moelelo o itseng wa molao.

[glossary]

[glossary-entry]

### Maikarabelo

Tiro ya go tlhalosa ditshwetso, go araba ba ba amegang, go amogela tlhatlhobo le go baakanya go palelwa fa go tlhokegang.

[/glossary-entry]

[glossary-entry]

### Mokgatlho

Batho ba ba ithaopang go rulaganya go dikologa boikaelelo jo bo tshwanang le go dumalana gore ba tla laola jang tiro ya bone e e kopanetsweng.

[/glossary-entry]

[glossary-entry]

### Bokgoni jo bo kopanetsweng

Tumelo ya setlhopha e e nang le maitemogelo, e e theilweng mo tirisong, ya gore se ka dira mmogo le go ama maemo a sona.

[/glossary-entry]

[glossary-entry]

### Tirisanommogo

Batho ba kopanya kitso e e farologaneng, bokgoni, le didirisiwa mo tirong e e abelanwang fa ba ntse ba boloka bokgoni jwa go sa dumalane, go gaisana le maatla le go gwetlha maemo a ba dirang mmogo mo go ona.

[/glossary-entry]

[glossary-entry]

### Tlwaelo

Didiriswa, kitso, mafaratlhatlha kgotsa bokgoni jo bo tshwerweng kgotsa jo bo laolwang ka maikaelelo a a tlhakanetsweng ka fa tlase ga melawana e e dirilweng kgotsa e e amogetsweng ke batho ba ba amegang.

[/glossary-entry]

[glossary-entry]

### Tlaleletso

Kamano e mo go yona dipharologano di nang le seabe mo bokgoning jo bo abelanwang ntle le go bewa mo maemong a a kwa godimo le a a kwa tlase.

[/glossary-entry]

[glossary-entry]

### Molaotheo

Melao e e laolang e e dumalanweng ke mokgatlho e e amanang le boikaelelo, botokololo, taolo, go dira ditshwetso, didirisiwa, boikarabelo, paakanyo le go tswalwa.

[/glossary-entry]

[glossary-entry]

### Romelo

Go naya motho kgotsa setheo taolo e e lekanyeditsweng ya go dira mo boemong jwa mokgatlho kwa ntle ga go fetisa taolo ya bofelo ya botokololo.

[/glossary-entry]

[glossary-entry]

### Tlhabololo ya temokerasi

Ntshetsopele e bulehileng ya maemo le bokgoni bo kopanetsweng boo ka tsona batho ba ka utlwisisa, ba busa, le ho fetola bophelo ba bona bo tlwaelehileng, ntle le ho nka tsela ya kgatelopele ya lefatshe kaofela.

[/glossary-entry]

[glossary-entry]

### Kganetso

Go tlhagisa go sa dumalane le tshwetso e e tshitshintsweng kgotsa e e leng teng, tlhaloso kgotsa mokgwa. Mo DDC e tsewa jaaka motswedi o o ka nnang teng wa kitso go na le go sa ikanyege.

[/glossary-entry]

[glossary-entry]

### Bolaodi

Kamano e e nnelang ruri e mo go yona batho bangwe kgotsa ditheo di dirisang maatla a a senang maikarabelo kgotsa a a kwa godimo mo go ba bangwe ka popego.

[/glossary-entry]

[glossary-entry]

### Doxa

Dikakanyo le mekgwa e e lebegang e le ya tlholego kgotsa e le e e bonalang sentle mo e leng gore gantsi e tlhagisiwa gape kwantle ga go ngangisanwa ka yone ka kelotlhoko.

[/glossary-entry]

[glossary-entry]

### Mokgatlho

Tirisanommogo magareng ga mekgatlho e e ikemetseng ka setheo se se abelanwang se dithata tsa sona di abetsweng ka tlhamalalo ke mekgatlho ya sona e e bopang.

[/glossary-entry]

[glossary-entry]

### Kgololesego ya go ikopanya

Kgololesego ya go bopa, go tsenela, go tshegetsa, kgotsa go tlogela mekgatlho le go dira mmogo le ba bangwe, go ikaegilwe ka ditshwanelo tsa molaotheo le molao o o maleba.

[/glossary-entry]

[glossary-entry]

### Tatelano ya maemo

Kamano e e rulagantsweng ya bogolo le go ikoba. Pharologano mo seabeng kgotsa mo boitseanapeng ga se tatelano ya maemo ka go itirisa; kgang ke gore maatla a rulagantswe jang.

[/glossary-entry]

[glossary-entry]

### Kakanyo mo tirisong

Ditsela tse ditheo le ditlwaelo di tswelelang go tlhaeletsana ka se se leng teng, se se tsewang se le botlhokwa, se se kgonegang le gore ke mefuta efe ya dikarolo tsa loago tse batho ba solofetsweng go di dira.

[/glossary-entry]

[glossary-entry]

### Tlhatlhobo e e ikemetseng

Tlhatlhobo e e dirwang ke batho ba ba ikemetseng ka nosi mo go lekaneng mo go ba go tlhatlhobiwang ditshwetso tsa bone, madi kgotsa tsamaiso.

[/glossary-entry]

[glossary-entry]

### Taelo

Maikaelelo, taolo le melelwane e e neetsweng moemedi, moemedi, komiti kgotsa motlhankedi.

[/glossary-entry]

[glossary-entry]

### Boipuso jwa leloko

Molawana wa gore taolo ya bofelo mo mokgatlhong e mo malokong a ona go na le boto ya ona, badiri, batlamedi ba matlole kgotsa baitseanape ba kwa ntle.

[/glossary-entry]

[glossary-entry]

### Thusano

Mokgatlho wa go dirisana o ka ona batho ba kopanyang bokgoni kgotsa didirisiwa go fitlhelela ditlhokego tse di abelanwang, go na le thuso e e lebileng go tswa mo bathusing go ya kwa baamogeladitshiamelo.

[/glossary-entry]

[glossary-entry]

### Ponatshegelo

Go dira mo ditheong tsa ga jaana dikamano tsa temokerasi le mefuta ya tirisanommogo e batho ba batlang go e dira gore e kgonege ka bophara.

[/glossary-entry]

[glossary-entry]

### Tiriso

Kamano e e ipoeletsang magareng ga tiro le go akanya e ka yona batho ba ranolang maemo, ba dira, ba ithuta mo ditlamoragong le go boeletsa se ba se dirang.

[/glossary-entry]

[glossary-entry]

### Marobalo a a utlwalang

Diphetolo tse di tlhokegang le tse di maleba tse di kgontshang motho yo o golafetseng go tsaya karolo ka go lekana fa dikgato tsa phitlhelelo ka kakaretso di sa lekana, ntle le go pega morwalo o o sa lekalekaneng.

[/glossary-entry]

[glossary-entry]

### Phasalatso

Phetogo ya ka boomo ya ka moo ditsompelo tsa dithoto, ditšhono, dipoelo kgotsa merwalo di abiwang ka teng, e ka tswa e le mo teng ga mokgatlho kgotsa ka dithulaganyo tse di anameng tsa loago le tsa setšhaba.

[/glossary-entry]

[glossary-entry]

### Boikaelelo jo bo tlhakanetsweng

Maikaelelo a a tshwanang a a nayang mokgatlho lebaka la go nna teng le go kaela tiriso ya one ya didirisiwa.

[/glossary-entry]

[glossary-entry]

### Botsayakarolo jo bo tshegediwang

Go tsaya karolo mo motho a dirisang thuso, tlhaloso, tshegetso ya tlhaeletsano, kgotsa thuso e nngwe fa a ntse a bolokile boemedi jwa gagwe le maemo a gagwe a sepolotiki.

[/glossary-entry]

[glossary-entry]

### Moakanyetso wa lefatshe lotlhe

Go tlhama ditikologo, tshedimosetso, dithulaganyo le didirisiwa gore di kgone go dirisiwa ke batho ba le bantsi ba ba mosola go tswa kwa tshimologong go na le go ikaega fela ka diphetogo tsa moragonyana tsa motho ka bongwe.

[/glossary-entry]

[glossary-entry]

### Mokgatlho wa boithaopo

Mokgatlho o o tlhamilweng ka tumalano magareng ga batho go na le ka go nna leloko le le patelediwang kgotsa go tlhamiwa pele ke puso.

[/glossary-entry]

[/glossary]

## Tsenela.

Thusa go tlhama le go leka Di-Common tsa Tlhabololo ya Temokerasi ka puisano, thanolo, tiriso e e mosola, le go akanya. Mekgatlho e tshwanetse go thusa go bopa letlhomeso le go gwetlha dikakanyo tsa lone.

Go tlhagisa kgatlhego ga go reye gore o nne leloko la porojeke ya puo kgotsa mokgatlho. Porojeke nngwe le nngwe e tshwanetse go dumalana le go tlhalosa dithulaganyo tsa yona tsa botokololo le go tsaya karolo.

## Ka ga lefelo leno.

Letlhomeso le la setšhaba le dirisa [Karolo II ya pampiri e tshweu e e dirang](/en/white-paper#part-ii). Pampiri e neelana ka mabaka a a feletseng, metswedi le dikgwetlho tse di botlhokwa tse di ka fa morago ga yone. Bobedi letlhomeso le didiriswa tsa lona tse di tshegetsang di nna di buletswe go tlhatlhobiwa le go boelediwa ka botsayakarolo.

Maikaelelo ke go nonotsha bokgoni jwa mekgatlho jwa go tlhaloganya, go busa, go botsa dipotso, le go itira sešwa, kwantle ga go ikaega ka polatefomo ya DDC kgotsa baitseanape ba yona. Go ithulaganya mmogo go tshwanetse gape go matlafatsa bokgoni jwa go gwetlha go tlhoka tekatekano go go anameng le go dira gore ditheo tsa puso di nne le maikarabelo.
