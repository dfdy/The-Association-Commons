## Ukwenza ikamva ngoku

Ipolitiki yokwenza ikamva ngoku ithetha ukuphila ubudlelwane neendlela zokuzilungiselela esifuna ukuzibona eluntwini, ngendlela esisebenzisana ngayo ngoku. Iindlela zibonakalisa iinjongo: ukuthatha inxaxheba ngokuzithandela, ukwenza izigqibo ngokubambisana nokuphendulana ngezenzo zethu.

I-The Democratic Development Commons ibonelela ngesikhokelo sokuzilungiselela ngokuzithandela abantu abanokusisebenzisa ngoku, ngokufunda ngokubambisana, ukuncedana, nokuzilawula ngentando yesininzi. Amalungu anokuxoxa ngemigaqo yaso, ayilungelelanise, aze ayiphuhlise kwimigaqo-siseko yawo nasekuzilungiseleleni kwemihla ngemihla, ethatha imigaqo-siseko yawo njengezivumelwano eziphilayo ezenza inxaxheba ibe nokwenzeka neziguqukayo ngamava ekwabelwana ngawo. Isixhobo esicwangcisiweyo sokuyila imigaqo-siseko kunye nethala leendlela zolawulo elisaphuhliswayo zenzelwe ukuxhasa le nkqubo, amalungu egcina igunya phezu kwemithetho ayamkelayo. Esi sikhokelo asiyomthetho ocetywayo, yaye ukusamkela akuthathi indawo yemithetho ekhoyo.

# isiXhosa

Isikhokelo esicetywayo asikaguqulelwa ngokupheleleyo kolu lwimi. Le yiprojekthi yolwimi ezimeleyo. Esi sikhokelo sisivumelwano sokuzilungiselela ngokuzithandela, asiyomthetho ocetywayo.

[/hero-intro]

Iprojekthi nganye yolwimi ilawulwa yi-stadgar yayo (umgaqosiseko okanye imithetho yombutho). Amalungu amisela iinjongo, indlela yokwenza izigqibo nobulungu ngokwaloo mithetho. Ukubonisa umdla apha akukwenzi ilungu ngokuzenzekelayo.

[section-layout label="06 / Thatha inxaxheba"]

## Bonisa umdla wokujoyina le projekthi

Isicelo sokuthatha inxaxheba kule projekthi yolwimi. Le fomu ibonisa umdla kuphela; iprojekthi imisela ubulungu ngokwe-stadgar yayo.

[/section-layout]
