## Ukwenza ikusasa libe khona manje

Ipolitiki yokwenza ikusasa libe khona manje isho ukwenza ubudlelwano nezindlela zokuhlela esifuna ukuzibona emphakathini ngendlela esisebenzisana ngayo manje. Izindlela zibonisa izinhloso: ukubamba iqhaza ngokuzithandela, ukuthatha izinqumo ngokuhlanganyela nokuphendulana ngezikwenzayo.

I-The Democratic Development Commons inikeza uhlaka lokuzihlela ngokuzithandela abantu abangalusebenzisa manje, ngokufunda ngokuhlanganyela, ukusizana, nokuzibusa ngentando yeningi. Amalungu angaxoxa ngezimiso zalo, azivumelanise nezimo zawo, futhi azithuthukise emithethwenisisekelo yawo nasekuzihleleni kwansuku zonke, ethatha imithethosisekelo yawo njengezivumelwano eziphilayo ezenza ukubamba iqhaza kube nokwenzeka futhi ziguquke ngokuhlangenwe nakho okwabelwanayo. Ithuluzi elihleliwe lokwakha imithethosisekelo nomtapo wezindlela zokuphatha osathuthukiswa kuhloswe ngakho ukusekela le nqubo, amalungu egcina igunya phezu kwemithetho ayamukelayo. Lolu hlaka aluwona umthetho ohlongozwayo, futhi ukulamukela akuthathi indawo yemithetho ekhona.

## Ukuhlangana kuqala nabantu.

Abantu kufanele bakhululeke ukuzihlanganisa ngezidingo, izinjongo, kanye nalokho okungenzeka, kanye nokuphatha izikhungo abazidalayo. Ukusebenzisana kusho ukusebenza ndawonye kuzo zonke izingxabano; ayidingi kuzwana noma ikhiphe ukungezwani, ukuphikisana noma inselele ehleliwe emandleni.

I-ejensi yentando yeningi ayinikezwa abazuzi abangenzi lutho ngochwepheshe, izinhlangano, izimakethe noma umbuso. Kuthuthukiswa ngamakhono abantu ahlangene okuqonda izimo zabo, ukwenza izinqumo, ukunakekelana, ukusebenzelana kwabaphathi, ukubekela inselele ukubusa, ukufunda ezenzweni kanye nokubuyekeza izinhlelo abahlala ngazo kanye nokusebenza ndawonye. Izikhungo zikahulumeni nokho zigcina izibopho zazo zokuhlinzeka ngamalungelo, izimpahla zomphakathi kanye nezinsizakalo zokuziphendulela.

IDemocratic Development Commons ihlinzeka ngohlaka olwabiwe lokwenza lokhu. Isebenzisa amasiko ahlukene aseNingizimu Afrika ezisebenzi, ezomphakathi, ukusizana, ezemfundo, ezenkolo, zabafundi, ezingomakhelwane kanye nezinye izinhlangano zokuzithandela; ngezinqubo eziwusizo ezithuthukiswe kwenye indawo; kanye nezibopho zomthethosisekelo zenkululeko, ukulingana, isithunzi, nokubamba iqhaza.

Ayichazi indlela eyodwa yenhlangano noma indlela yentuthuko. Izinhlangano ezehlukene zingagcina izinhloso zazo, amagama, amasiko, nezinhlaka ngenkathi zibuza imibuzo evamile yentando yeningi mayelana namandla, ukubamba iqhaza, ulwazi, izinsiza, ukuziphendulela kanye noshintsho.

Izinhlangano zihlala zinesibopho sokuhlonza ifomu lomthetho nemisebenzi ethile ehambisana nomsebenzi wazo.

[section-layout label="02 / Izisekelo"]

## Izimiso zokuhlela eziyishumi.

Lezi zimiso zokuhlela zivulekele izingxoxo, ukuhlolwa, kanye nokucwengwa. Banikeza imibuzo eyabiwe yezinhlobo ezahlukene zokuhlangana, esikhundleni semodeli yenhlangano eyodwa.

[subsection number="01"]

### Inkululeko kanye nenhlangano yokuzithandela

Abantu bangase bakhe, bajoyine, basekele, noma bashiye izinhlangano ngaphandle kokulawula kwangaphandle okungadingekile. Okulindelwe ubulungu, izibopho kanye nemiphumela kufanele kuqondakala ngaphambi kokuba abantu bazibophezele.

[/subsection]

[subsection number="02"]

### Ukuma okulinganayo

Ilungu ngalinye lithathwa njengomuntu okwaziyo ukucabanga, ukwahlulela nokubamba iqhaza. Umehluko olwazini, ikhono, ulwazi, isikhundla, noma umthwalo wemfanelo awudali izigaba eziphakeme neziphansi zamalungu.

[/subsection]

[subsection number="03"]

### Ukuzibusa ngokuhlanganyela

Amalungu agcina igunya elikhulu phezu kwenjongo yenhlangano, umthethosisekelo, kanye nezinsiza ezinkulu ezabiwe. Abameleli nochwepheshe basebenzisa amandla abawaphathisiwe esikhundleni sokuthatha isikhundla se-ejensi yamalungu.

[/subsection]

[subsection number="04"]

### Umehluko ngaphandle kokubusa

Intando yeningi ayidingi ukufana. Umehluko kufanele ukhulise umthamo weqoqo kunokuba uqine ebudlelwaneni bokuyala, ukuncika, ukukhishwa ngaphandle noma ukuphakama.

[/subsection]

[subsection number="05"]

### Ukubamba iqhaza okubonakalayo nokufinyeleleka

Ilungelo elisemthethweni lokubamba iqhaza alanele lapho ulimi, ukufunda nokubhala, ukukhubazeka, ezokuthutha, izindleko, ukunakekelwa, umsebenzi, ukuphepha, ubuchwepheshe, noma isimo senhlalo kwenza ukubamba iqhaza kungasebenziseki. Inhlangano yentando yeningi kufanele isuse imigoqo ngokusebenzisa idizayini yendawo yonke, indawo yokuhlala efanele, nezindlela eziningi zokubamba iqhaza.

[/subsection]

[subsection number="06"]

### Ukufunda ngokuhlanganyela kanye nokubuza

Inhlangano iyafundisa. Amalungu afunda ngemihlangano, imikhankaso, ucwaningo, ukungqubuzana, isabelomali, ukunakekelwa, umsebenzi owenziwayo, kanye nokuzindla. Uchwepheshe kufanele bakhulise ukuqonda okuhlangene kunokuba bakhe ukubusa phezu kolwazi.

[/subsection]

[subsection number="07"]

### Ukuphatha okwabiwe, ukusizana, nokuzibophezela kwezemvelo

Imali, ulwazi, izikhala, izinto zokusebenza, isikhathi, ukunakekelwa, nezinye izinsiza okwabelwana ngazo kufanele zifeze izinhloso okuvunyelwene ngazo kunokunothisa okuyimfihlo. Izinhlangano kufanele zihlele ukwesekwa okufanayo lapho kufanele futhi zicabangele imiphumela yezemvelo yemisebenzi yazo. Ukusizana okuhlanganyelwe kungenza ukubamba iqhaza nobumbano kwenzeke, kodwa akufanele kufihle ukungalingani okubanzi, kuthathe indawo yokwabiwa kabusha noma kukhulule izikhungo zomphakathi ezibophweni ezisemthethweni.

[/subsection]

[subsection number="08"]

### Amandla anesibopho, ukuphikisa, nokubuyekeza

Isiphathimandla esiphathisiwe kufanele sihlale sibonakala, silinganiselwe, sichazeka, futhi sibuyekezwa. Amalungu adinga izindlela ezingokoqobo zokubuza izinqumo, ukuveza izikhundla zabantu abambalwa, ukubekela abaholi inselelo futhi afune ukucutshungulwa ngokuzimela ngaphandle kokuziphindiselela.

[/subsection]

[subsection number="09"]

### Ukuzimela kanye nomfelandawonye

Izinhlangano kufanele zilawule izinhloso zazo ngenkathi zihlala zikhululekile ukubambisana. Izinhlaka ezinkulu kufanele zithole igunya kososeshini ababambe iqhaza ngokudluliswa okucacile kunokuba ziqongelele amandla angadingekile esikhungweni.

[/subsection]

[subsection number="10"]

### Ukuthuthukiswa okuvulekile kanye ne-reflexive

Alikho iladi elivela emhlabeni wonke elisuka enhlanganweni “yendabuko” iye kweyanamuhla. Ososeshini kufanele bahlole ukuthi imikhuba yabo yenza kube nokwenzeka yini, ukuthi izithakazelo zikabani ezisizayo, iziphi izinto ezicatshangwayo ezithathwa njengezibalulekile kanye nokuthi yikuphi okuhlangenwe nakho okusikisela ukuthi kufanele kushintshe.

[/subsection]

[/section-layout]


[section-layout label="03 / Uhlaka" surface="shaded"]

## Uhlaka lokuhlela.

Lezi zibopho zihumusha izimiso zibe yimithethosisekelo kanye nezinqubo zansuku zonke amalungu angaqonda, azisebenzise, futhi azibuyekeze ndawonye.

[subsection number="01"]

### Qala ngenhloso ehlanganyelwe

Vumelanani ukuthi yini ehlanganisa abantu, abathintwa umsebenzi, lokho amalungu anethemba lokukufeza, yiziphi izimo afuna ukuzishintsha nokuthi inhloso ingase ithuthuke kanjani.

[/subsection]

[subsection number="02"]

### Yenza umthethosisekelo ube yisivumelwano sokusebenza

Beka ubulungu, ukwenza izinqumo, amandla aphathisiwe, izinsiza, ukuziphendulela, ukungqubuzana, ukuchitshiyelwa, nokuvalwa ngolimi namafomethi amalungu angawasebenzisa. Umthethosisekelo kufanele ubuyekezwe kunokuba uthathwe njengombhalo wokusungula onyamalala ube yifayela. Ithuluzi elihleliwe [I-Association Constitution Drafting Tool](/zu/stadgar) lizohlose ukuhlinzeka ngohlaka lokuqala lwamalungu ukuze axoxe, ajwayelane, futhi awamukele; ayikatholakali. Ukuthuthukiswa [umtapo wolwazi wephethini yokubusa](/en/governance-patterns) unikeza izinketho ezingakahloliwe zocwaningo nokubuyekezwa okuhlangene.

[/subsection]

[subsection number="03"]

### Gcina izinqumo ezibalulekile namalungu

Thola ukuthi yiziphi izinqumo ezidinga ubulungu ngokwazo nokuthi yiziphi ezingadluliswa. Izinguquko ezibalulekile enjongweni, emithethweni yomthethosisekelo, amandla obuholi noma izimpahla ezinkulu ezabiwe kufanele zibuyele kumalungu.

[/subsection]

[subsection number="04"]

### Thumela omunye esikhundleni sepolitiki

Nikeza abameleli igunya elanele lokwenza umsebenzi odingekayo, kodwa bachaze igunya labo, ubude besikhathi, imisebenzi yokubika, ukubuyekezwa, kanye nokususwa. Ukudlulisela amandla kufanele kulandele ubudlelwano obuqhubekayo:

** igunya → isenzo → umbiko → ukuhlolisisa → ukubuyekeza**

[/subsection]

[subsection number="05"]

### Yenza ukubamba iqhaza kusebenziseke

Dizayina izaziso, imihlangano, imibhalo, ukuvota, kanye nokuxhumana phakathi kwabantu okumele bakusebenzise. Nikeza ukufinyelela kolimi okufanelekile, amafomethi afinyelelekayo, ukubamba iqhaza okusekelwayo kanye nendawo yokuhlala efanele ngaphandle kokudalula komuntu siqu noma kokwelashwa okungadingekile.

[/subsection]

[subsection number="06"]

### Sabalalisa ulwazi namandla enhlangano

Bhala umsebenzi obalulekile, yabelana ngamakhono kanye nokulandelana kwepulani. Ezezimali, ubuchwepheshe, ukugqugquzela, ubudlelwano bangaphandle, kanye nenkumbulo yenhlangano akufanele kugxile ngokungadingekile kubantu abangabalulekile.

[/subsection]

[subsection number="07"]

### Vikela ukuhlangana kwentando yeningi nokungaboni ngaso linye

Dala amathuba asimeme ukuze abantu bahlukane phakathi komphakathi ukuze basebenzisane ngezinjongo ezihlanganyelwe, ngaphandle kokwenza sengathi ukungalingani kwesakhiwo kunyamalele. Phatha ukungavumelani nokucabanga okuncane njengemithombo yolwazi esikhundleni sobufakazi bokungathembeki.

[/subsection]

[subsection number="08"]

### Umphathi wabelane ngezinsiza futhi azijwayeze ukusizana

Vumelana ukuthi imali, izimpahla, ulwazi, ukunakekelwa, izikhala, nezinye izinsiza ezivamile zinikelwa, zisetshenziswa, zirekhodwa futhi zidluliselwe kanjani. Hlukanisa ubunzima nokungaziphathi kahle futhi ugweme ukubekela abantu eceleni buthule ngoba abakwazi ukuhlangabezana nenkambiso ecatshangwayo. Lapho izidingo zivela ezimeni ezibanzi zesakhiwo, buza ukuthi ingabe impendulo efanele iwukusizana, ukusabalalisa kabusha, isimangalo somphakathi, noma inhlanganisela ethile yalokhu.

[/subsection]

[subsection number="09"]

### Sungula ukuhlola okuzimele

Abantu abasebenzisa igunya eliphathisiwe akufanele babe nesibopho sokuzibuyekeza bona ngokwabo. Amalungu kufanele aqoke ababuyekezi abazimele abafanele isikali kanye nezingozi zenhlangano.

[/subsection]

[subsection number="10"]

### Gcina amarekhodi athembekile futhi afinyeleleka kalula

Izinqumo zamarekhodi, izibopho kanye nolwazi lwezezimali kumafomu amalungu angawahlola. Lapho kufaneleka, amalungu kufanele aqinisekise ukuthi amarekhodi omhlangano abonisa ngokunembile lokho okunqunyiwe.

[/subsection]

[subsection number="11"]

### Bhekana nokungqubuzana nokulimala ngendlela efanele

Hlukanisa ukungezwani nokuziphatha okulimazayo. Nikeza izindlela ezifinyelelekayo zokuveza ukukhathazeka, ezinye izindlela lapho abantu abanamandla bethinteka, amathuba okuphendula, amakhambi alinganayo kanye nokubuyekeza.

[/subsection]

[subsection number="12"]

### Vikela ukuzimela kwenhlangano

Yenza izimo ezinkulu zoxhaso, ubambiswano, ubudlelwano bezepolitiki kanye nokuncika kwezikhungo kubonakale. Amalungu kufanele akwazi ukwahlulela ukuthi ukwesekwa kwangaphandle kuqondisa kabusha izinto ezibalulekile, inkulumo noma ukuphatha.

[/subsection]

[subsection number="13"]

### Funda ngesenzo nokubuyekeza ngezikhathi ezithile

Sebenzisa isenzo esihlangene, imiphumela yaso kanye nokuzindla njengemithombo yolwazi. Ngezikhathi ezithile hlola amandla, ubudlelwano bentando yeningi, imibono kanye nokuqagela okuthathiwe, uhlu lozalo nokungafundi, ukubamba iqhaza kanye nokufinyelela, bese ubuyisela lokho okufundiwe esenzweni.

[/subsection]

[subsection number="14"]

### Federate ngaphandle kokufakwa endaweni eyodwa okungadingekile

Lapho ukubambisana ngezinga elikhulu kuwusizo, cacisa ukuthi yimaphi amandla ahlala esendaweni futhi yimaphi adluliselwe phezulu. Izithunywa kufanele ziphathe iziyalezo futhi zibike.

[/subsection]

[subsection number="15"]

### Sebenzisa umthetho njengephansi kanye nensiza

Khomba ifomu lomthetho kanye nemithetho eqondene nomsebenzi ethile ehambisana nenhlangano. Hlangana namalungelo asebenzayo, ukubhalisa, ukubika, ukulingana, ubumfihlo, intela, ukuqashwa, imisebenzi yezezimali noma eminye ngaphandle kokudida ukuthobela umthetho nekhwalithi yentando yeningi. I- [legal matrix](/en/legal-matrix) insiza yocwaningo yesikhashana; amarekhodi ayo okuqala alindele ukuqinisekiswa.

[/subsection]

[section-layout label="04 / Ngokuzijwayeza"]

## Ukusebenzisa ukuzibusa.

Izinhlangano ezihlukene zingasebenzisa amagama ahlukene, amahhovisi, namafomu emihlangano. Khetha izinhlelo ezihambisana nomsebenzi, kuyilapho ugcina igunya liziphendulela kumalungu.

[subsection number="01"]

### Amalungu

Amalungu akha isisekelo sentando yeningi sososeshini. Amalungelo abo, izibopho, ukwamukelwa, ukumiswa, ukuphuma, kanye nezindlela zokubamba iqhaza kufanele kucace.

[/subsection]

[subsection number="02"]

### Umhlangano ojwayelekile noma umhlangano

Isigcawu esiyinhloko sokuthatha izinqumo sobulungu kufanele sibhekane nezindaba ezigcinelwe amalungu, samukele imibiko, sibuyekeze igunya eliphathisiwe futhi sichibiyele imithetho elawulayo. Igama eliqondile, imvamisa nenqubo kungahluka.

[/subsection]

[subsection number="03"]

### Ibhodi, ikomidi noma izikhulu eziphathisiwe

Ibhodi noma umgwamanda ofanayo wenza umsebenzi phakathi kwemihlangano yamalungu ngaphakathi kwamandla achaziwe. Ukuba khona kwayo akufanele kuguqule isibopho sokuphatha esiphathisiwe sibe ubukhosi phezu kukasoseshini.

[/subsection]

[subsection number="04"]

### Amaqembu asebenzayo nabagqugquzeli

Izinhlangano zingakha amaqembu esikhashana noma aqhubekayo emisebenzi ethile. Amagunya abo, izinsiza, kanye nobudlelwano bokubika kufanele bucace, futhi ukugqugquzelwa kufanele kusekele ukubamba iqhaza kunokusungula igunya lobuhlakani.

[/subsection]

[subsection number="05"]

### Ababuyekezi abazimele

Amalungu aqoka abantu abazimele ngokwanele kulabo abaphethe inhlangano ukuze babuyekeze izimali, igunya, inqubo, ukungqubuzana kwezintshisekelo kanye nezinye izibopho ezibonakalayo. Izinhlangano ezinkulu noma ezilawulwayo zingase zidinge ukuhlolwa kochwepheshe ngaphezu kokubuyekezwa kwentando yeningi.

[/subsection]

[subsection number="06"]

### Umthethosisekelo, amarekhodi kanye nenkumbulo yenhlangano

Amalungu kufanele akwazi ukufinyelela emithethweni ebusayo, izinqumo ezibalulekile, ulwazi lwezezimali, namarekhodi adingekayo ukuze kuqondwe inhlangano. Inkumbulo yenhlangano kufanele isinde ekushintsheni kobuholi.

[/subsection]

[subsection number="07"]

### Ukuqala inhlangano

Uhlu lokuhlola lokuqala lokuxoxa nokuzivumelanisa ndawonye:

1. Khomba abantu, inhloso okwabelwana ngayo kanye nabantu abathintwe umsebenzi.
2. Xoxa ngobulungu, ukubamba iqhaza, ulimi, ukufinyelela kokukhubazeka kanye nezithiyo zezinto ezibonakalayo ngaphambi kokulungisa izinqubo.
3. Khomba ifomu lomthetho kanye nemisebenzi ethile engasebenza.
4. Vumelanani ukuthi yiziphi izinqumo ezisele namalungu futhi ezingadluliswa.
5. Uhlaka futhi wamukele ngokuhlanganyela umthethosisekelo osebenzisekayo.
6. Khetha abameleli nababuyekezi abazimele, chaza iziqondiso zabo futhi uhlele ukulandelana.
7. Vumelana ukuthi imali, izimpahla, amarekhodi, ubumfihlo, nokungqubuzana kwenzuzo kuzosingathwa kanjani.
8. Rekhoda futhi uqinisekise izinqumo zokusungula kanye nokuqokwa.
9. Hlela ukubhanga, ukubhaliswa, ukugunyazwa kwentela noma izimvume ezilawulwayo lapho kudingeka khona.
10. Qala umsebenzi, bese ubuyekeza ukuthi imithetho ikhiqiza ini bese uyichibiyela uma kunesidingo.

[/subsection]

[subsection number="08"]

### Izibuyekezo ezinhlanu eziphindelelayo zentando yeningi

Izinhlangano zingasebenzisa izinqubo ezinhlanu zokubuyekeza ezimfushane ngezikhathi ezithile:

**Ubukhosi namandla:** Ukuphi ukugxilisa amandla, futhi yimaphi amakhono amasha noma amathuba avelayo?

**Ubudlelwano beDemocratic:** Ubani obambe iqhaza, ubani ongaphikisa, amaqembu ahlukene ahlobana kanjani, futhi ingabe ukuvumelana kufihla ukungalingani?

**I-Ideology kanye ne-doxa:** Yini inhlangano eklomelisa ngayo, iyenza ibe yinto evamile noma yenza kubonakale kungenakwenzeka, okuhlanganisa nokuqagela okungekho muntu oke wacabanga ukukubuza?

**Uhlu lozalo kanye nokungafundi:** Izigaba zethu nezinqubo zethu zavelaphi, futhi ingabe sidida ubungcweti, isikali noma isiko elizuzwe njengefa nesidingo noma inqubekelaphambili?

**Ukubamba iqhaza nokufinyelela:** Ubani ongakwazi ukusebenzisa izinqubo ezijwayelekile, yiziphi izithiyo ezikhiqiza lokho kukhishwa, futhi yiziphi izinguquko ezidingekayo?

Lezi zibuyekezo kufanele ziholele ezinqumweni, izibopho ezabelwe futhi, lapho kufanele, isinyathelo esihlangene ngale kwenhlangano kunokuba kube ukubonakaliswa okungokomfanekiso.

[/subsection]

[section-layout label="05 / Uhlu lwamagama"]

## Amagama wokuhlela ndawonye.

Izincazelo zolimi olulula ezisetshenziswe ku-DDC. Lokhu akuthatheli indawo yezincazelo zomthetho zobuchwepheshe lapho igama linencazelo ethile esemthethweni.

[glossary]

[glossary-entry]

### Ukuziphendulela

Umsebenzi wokuchaza izinqumo, ukuphendula labo abathintekayo, ukwamukela ukucutshungulwa nokulungisa ukwehluleka lapho kudingeka.

[/glossary-entry]

[glossary-entry]

### Ukuhlangana

Abantu abahlela ngokuzithandela ngenhloso eyabiwe futhi bavumelane ukuthi bangawulawula kanjani umsebenzi wabo weqoqo.

[/glossary-entry]

[glossary-entry]

### Ukusebenza ngokuhlanganyela

Inkolelo yeqembu enesipiliyoni, esekelwe ekusebenzeni, yokuthi lingakwazi ukusebenzisana futhi lithinte izimo zalo.

[/glossary-entry]

[glossary-entry]

### Ukubambisana

Abantu bahlanganisa ulwazi olwahlukene, amakhono, kanye nezinsiza esenzweni okwabelwana ngaso kuyilapho begcina ikhono lokungavumelani, lokubanga amandla kanye nokubekela inselele izimo abasebenza ngaphansi kwazo.

[/glossary-entry]

[glossary-entry]

### Commons

Izinsiza, ulwazi, ingqalasizinda noma amakhono aphethwe noma abuswe ngezinhloso ezabiwe ngaphansi kwemithetho eyenziwe noma eyamukelwe abantu abathintekayo.

[/glossary-entry]

[glossary-entry]

### Ukuhambisana

Ubudlelwano lapho umehluko unomthelela emandleni okwabelwana ngawo ngaphandle kokufakwa ezikhundleni eziphezulu neziphansi.

[/glossary-entry]

[glossary-entry]

### Umthethosisekelo

Imithetho yenhlangano elawulayo okuvunyelenwe ngayo mayelana nenjongo, ubulungu, igunya, ukwenza izinqumo, izinsiza, ukuziphendulela, ukuchitshiyelwa, nokuvalwa.

[/glossary-entry]

[glossary-entry]

### Ukuthunyelwa

Ukunikeza umuntu noma indikimba igunya elilinganiselwe lokumelela inhlangano ngaphandle kokudlulisa igunya lokugcina lobulungu.

[/glossary-entry]

[glossary-entry]

### Ukuthuthukiswa kwentando yeningi

Ukuthuthukiswa okuvulelekayo kwezimo namandla eqoqo abantu abangaqonda, baphathe, futhi baguqule ukuphila kwabo okufanayo, ngaphandle kokuthatha indlela yomhlaba wonke yenqubekelaphambili.

[/glossary-entry]

[glossary-entry]

### Ukuphikisa

Uzwakalise ukungavumelani nesinqumo esihlongozwayo noma esikhona, ukuhumusha noma ukwenza. Ku-DDC ithathwa njengomthombo wolwazi ongaba khona esikhundleni sokungathembeki.

[/glossary-entry]

[glossary-entry]

### Ukubusa

Ubudlelwano obuhlala isikhathi eside lapho abanye abantu noma izikhungo zisebenzisa amandla angenakuziphendulela noma aphakeme ngokwesakhiwo kunabanye.

[/glossary-entry]

[glossary-entry]

### Doxa

Ukuqagela kanye nezinqubo ezibonakala zingokwemvelo noma zisobala kangangokuthi ngokuvamile zikhiqizwa ngaphandle kokuphikiswana ngakho.

[/glossary-entry]

[glossary-entry]

### Inhlangano

Ukubambisana phakathi kwezinhlangano ezizimele ngokusebenzisa indikimba eyabiwe amandla ayo adluliselwe ngokusobala izinhlangano zawo ezizimele.

[/glossary-entry]

[glossary-entry]

### Inkululeko yokuzihlanganisa

Inkululeko yokusungula, ukujoyina, ukusekela, noma ukushiya izinhlangano nokusebenza ngokuhlanganyela nabanye, kuncike kumalungelo omthethosisekelo kanye nomthetho osebenzayo.

[/glossary-entry]

[glossary-entry]

### Ukubusa

Ubudlelwano obuhleliwe bokuphakama nokuzithoba. Umehluko endimeni noma ochwepheshe akuwona uhlelo oluzenzakalelayo; indaba iwukuthi amandla akheke kanjani.

[/glossary-entry]

[glossary-entry]

### I-Ideology ekusebenzeni

Izindlela izikhungo kanye nezinqubo ezihlala zikhuluma ngazo ngalokho okukhona, lokho okuyigugu, okungenzeka kanye nezinhlobo zezindima zomphakathi okulindeleke ukuba abantu bazibambe.

[/glossary-entry]

[glossary-entry]

### Ukubuyekeza okuzimele

Ukucutshungulwa okwenziwa abantu abazimele ngokwanele kulabo abahlolisiswa izinqumo zabo, izimali zabo noma ukuphatha.

[/glossary-entry]

[glossary-entry]

### Igunya

Inhloso, igunya kanye nemikhawulo enikezwe ummeleli, isithunywa, ikomidi noma osesikhundleni.

[/glossary-entry]

[glossary-entry]

### Ubukhosi belungu

Umgomo wokuthi igunya eliphelele phezu kwenhlangano likumalungu ayo esikhundleni sebhodi, abasebenzi, abaxhasi bezimali noma ochwepheshe bangaphandle.

[/glossary-entry]

[glossary-entry]

### Usizo oluhlanganyelwe

Inhlangano ebuyiselanayo lapho abantu behlanganisa khona amandla noma izinsiza ukuze kuhlangatshezwane nezidingo ezabiwe, kunezinhlangano ezisiza umphakathi ezisuka kosizo ziye kwabazuzi.

[/glossary-entry]

[glossary-entry]

### Ukufanekisa

Ukuzijwayeza ezikhungweni zamanje ubudlelwano bentando yeningi kanye nezindlela zokubambisana abantu abafuna ukuzenza ukuthi zenzeke kabanzi.

[/glossary-entry]

[glossary-entry]

### I-Praxis

Ubudlelwano obuqhubekayo phakathi kwesenzo nokuzindla lapho abantu behumusha izimo, benze okuthile, bafunde emiphumeleni futhi babuyekeze abakwenzayo.

[/glossary-entry]

[glossary-entry]

### Indawo yokuhlala enengqondo

Ukulungiswa okudingekayo nokufanelekile okuvumela umuntu okhubazekile ukuba abambe iqhaza ngokulinganayo lapho izindlela zokufinyeleleka ezivamile zingenele, ngaphandle kokubeka umthwalo ongalingani.

[/glossary-entry]

[glossary-entry]

### Ukusabalalisa kabusha

Ushintsho lwamabomu endleleni izinsiza ezibonakalayo, amathuba, izinzuzo noma imithwalo esabalaliswa ngayo, kungaba ngaphakathi kwenhlangano noma ngezinhlelo ezibanzi zomphakathi nezomphakathi.

[/glossary-entry]

[glossary-entry]

### Inhloso eyabiwe

Inhloso efanayo enikeza inhlangano isizathu sokuba khona futhi iqondise ukusetshenziswa kwayo kwezinsiza.

[/glossary-entry]

[glossary-entry]

### Ukubamba iqhaza okusekelwe

Ukubamba iqhaza lapho umuntu esebenzisa khona usizo, ukuhumusha, ukwesekwa kokuxhumana, noma olunye usizo kuyilapho egcine i-ejensi yakhe kanye nokuma kwezombusazwe.

[/glossary-entry]

[glossary-entry]

### Idizayini yendawo yonke

Ukuklama izindawo, ulwazi, izinqubo namathuluzi ukuze asetshenziswe uhla olubanzi kakhulu lwabantu kusukela ekuqaleni kunokuthembela kuphela ekulungiseni kwakamuva komuntu ngamunye.

[/glossary-entry]

[glossary-entry]

### Inhlangano yokuzithandela

Inhlangano eyakhiwe ngesivumelwano phakathi kwabantu esikhundleni sobulungu obuyimpoqo noma ukudalwa kwangaphambili nguhulumeni.

[/glossary-entry]

[/glossary]
