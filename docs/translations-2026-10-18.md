# Written-language translation update · 18 October 2026

English remains the canonical source. The full proposal, Association Constitution
Drafting Tool description, and donation information were translated into Afrikaans,
isiNdebele, Sepedi, siSwati, Sesotho, Setswana, Xitsonga, Tshivenda, isiXhosa, and isiZulu.
Kaaps uses an Afrikaans-based adaptation, explicitly labelled as needing Kaaps-speaker
review. This is a draft, not a claim of an independently validated Kaaps translation.
The SASL edition and English source pages were not replaced.

Machine drafts were generated using Google Translate from public source prose only.
No visitor submissions, account details, or credentials were sent. Runtime forms use
bundled translations and do not contact that service. Existing translated prefiguration
wording and its show/hide labels were retained because that English section was unchanged.
Structural shortcodes and link destinations were preserved; local drafting-tool links
point to the corresponding language edition. Each page records its English source
and source-file SHA-256 for subsequent review.

Machine-translation notices remain at the top of all translated pages. The notices
link to English and invite translation assistance. Human linguistic review, including
legal and governance terminology, remains outstanding across the new drafts.

Interest forms are included in every translated proposal, with translated labels,
consent, validation, and status text. Private records use the existing restricted
storage and retain the actual source route and language code. The canonical privacy
policy remains English. A subsequent update adds the English interest form to the SASL proposal; signed media remains unavailable.

Verification covers matching proposal shortcode structure, section/glossary counts,
links, translation notices, unchanged SASL, public rendering, form labels and error
focus, submission metadata, same-session route checks, exports without form tokens,
and automated form accessibility checks. The existing English form regression
checks also passed, and synthetic submissions were removed. These checks do not validate translation
meaning or establish WCAG conformance. Screen-reader and native-speaker review remain
outstanding.
