## Ukwenza ikamva ngoku

Ipolitiki yokwenza ikamva ngoku ithetha ukuphila ubudlelwane neendlela zokuzilungiselela esifuna ukuzibona eluntwini, ngendlela esisebenzisana ngayo ngoku. Iindlela zibonakalisa iinjongo: ukuthatha inxaxheba ngokuzithandela, ukwenza izigqibo ngokubambisana nokuphendulana ngezenzo zethu.

I-The Democratic Development Commons ibonelela ngesikhokelo sokuzilungiselela ngokuzithandela abantu abanokusisebenzisa ngoku, ngokufunda ngokubambisana, ukuncedana, nokuzilawula ngentando yesininzi. Amalungu anokuxoxa ngemigaqo yaso, ayilungelelanise, aze ayiphuhlise kwimigaqo-siseko yawo nasekuzilungiseleleni kwemihla ngemihla, ethatha imigaqo-siseko yawo njengezivumelwano eziphilayo ezenza inxaxheba ibe nokwenzeka neziguqukayo ngamava ekwabelwana ngawo. Isixhobo esicwangcisiweyo sokuyila imigaqo-siseko kunye nethala leendlela zolawulo elisaphuhliswayo zenzelwe ukuxhasa le nkqubo, amalungu egcina igunya phezu kwemithetho ayamkelayo. Esi sikhokelo asiyomthetho ocetywayo, yaye ukusamkela akuthathi indawo yemithetho ekhoyo.

## Ubudlelwane buqala ngabantu.

Abantu kufuneka bakhululeke ukuba bazibandakanye kwiimfuno ekwabelwana ngazo, iinjongo, kunye nezinto ezinokwenzeka, kunye nokulawula amaziko abawadalayo. Intsebenziswano ithetha ukusebenza kunye kuzo zonke iiyantlukwano; ayifuni imvisiswano okanye ikhuphe ukungavisisani, ukhuphiswano okanye umngeni olungelelanisiweyo kulawulo.

I-arhente yedemokhrasi ayinikwa abaxhamli abangenzi nto ziingcali, imibutho, iimarike okanye urhulumente. Ikhula ngezakhono zabantu ezidibeneyo zokuqonda iimeko zabo, ukwenza izigqibo, ukukhathalelana, igosa ekwabelwana ngalo ngezibonelelo, umngeni wolawulo, ukufunda kwizenzo kunye nokuhlaziya amalungiselelo abahlala kunye nokusebenza kunye. Amaziko karhulumente nangona kunjalo ayazigcina izibophelelo zawo zokubonelela ngamalungelo, iimpahla zoluntu kunye neenkonzo zokuphendula.

IDemocratic Development Commons ibonelela ngesakhelo ekwabelwana ngaso sokwenza oku. Ithathela kwizithethe ezahlukeneyo zoMzantsi Afrika zabasebenzi, ezoluntu, zokuncedana, imfundo, inkolo, abafundi, ubumelwane kunye neminye imibutho esebenza ngokuzithandela; kwiinkqubo eziluncedo eziphuhliswe kwenye indawo; kunye nezibophelelo zomgaqo-siseko kwinkululeko, ukulingana, isidima, kunye nokuthatha inxaxheba.

Ayichazi indlela enye yombutho okanye indlela yophuhliso. Imibutho eyahlukeneyo inokugcina iinjongo zabo, amagama, izithethe, kunye nezakhiwo ngelixa bebuza imibuzo eqhelekileyo yedemokhrasi malunga namandla, ukuthatha inxaxheba, ulwazi, izixhobo, ukuphendula kunye notshintsho.

Imibutho ihlala inoxanduva lokuchonga ifom esemthethweni kunye nemisebenzi ethile ehambelana nomsebenzi wayo.

[section-layout label="02 / Iziseko"]

## Imigaqo yolungiselelo elishumi.

Le migaqo yolungiselelo ivulelekile kwingxoxo, uvavanyo, kunye nokuphuculwa. Banika imibuzo ekwabelwana ngayo kwiindidi ezahlukeneyo zombutho, endaweni yemodeli yombutho omnye.

[subsection number="01"]

### Inkululeko kunye nombutho wokuzithandela

Abantu banokubumba, bajoyine, baxhase, okanye bashiye imibutho ngaphandle kolawulo lwangaphandle olungeyomfuneko. Ulindelo lobulungu, uxanduva kunye neziphumo kufuneka ziqondeke phambi kokuba abantu bazibophelele.

[/subsection]

[subsection number="02"]

### Ukuma ngokulinganayo

Ilungu ngalinye liza kuye njengomntu okwaziyo ukucinga, ukugweba kunye nokuthatha inxaxheba. Umahluko kulwazi, ubuchule, amava, isikhundla, okanye uxanduva aludali udidi oluphezulu noluphantsi lwamalungu.

[/subsection]

[subsection number="03"]

### Ukuzilawula ngokudibeneyo

Amalungu agcina igunya eliphezulu kwinjongo yombutho, umgaqo-siseko, kunye nezixhobo eziphambili ekwabelwana ngazo. Abameli kunye neengcali basebenzisa amagunya anikezelweyo endaweni yokuthatha indawo ye-arhente yobulungu.

[/subsection]

[subsection number="04"]

### Umahluko ngaphandle kokulawula

Idemokhrasi ayifuni okufanayo. Iiyantlukwano kufuneka zandise umthamo odibeneyo endaweni yokuqina kubudlelwane bomyalelo, ukuxhomekeka, ukungabandakanyi okanye ukongama.

[/subsection]

[subsection number="05"]

### Ukuthatha inxaxheba okubonakalayo kunye nokufikeleleka

Ilungelo elisesikweni lokuthatha inxaxheba alanelanga apho ulwimi, ukufunda nokubhala, ukukhubazeka, izithuthi, iindleko, inkathalo, umsebenzi, ukhuseleko, ubuchwepheshe, okanye iwonga lentlalo yenza ukuba uthatho-nxaxheba lungasetyenziswa. Umbutho wedemokhrasi kufuneka isuse imiqobo ngoyilo lwendalo iphela, indawo yokuhlala efanelekileyo, kunye neendlela ezininzi zokuthatha inxaxheba.

[/subsection]

[subsection number="06"]

### Ukufunda ngokudibeneyo kunye nemibuzo

Ulungelelwaniso luyafundisa. Amalungu afunda ngeentlanganiso, amaphulo, uphando, impixano, uhlahlo lwabiwo-mali, ukhathalelo, umsebenzi owenziwayo, kunye nokucamngca. Ubuchwephesha kufuneka bandise ukuqonda okuhlangeneyo endaweni yokuseka ulwazi kuphela.

[/subsection]

[subsection number="07"]

### Ukwabelana ngobugosa, ukuncedana, kunye noxanduva lwendalo

Imali, ulwazi, izithuba, izixhobo, ixesha, ukhathalelo, kunye nezinye izixhobo ekwabelwana ngazo kufuneka zifezekise iinjongo ekuvunyelwene ngazo endaweni yokutyebisa kwabucala. Imibutho kufuneka iququzelele inkxaso yokubuyiselana apho kufanelekileyo kwaye ithathele ingqalelo iziphumo zezinto eziphilayo neendawo zazo zokuhlala. Ukuncedana kunokwenza ukuba kubekho uthatho-nxaxheba nobumbano olubonakalayo, kodwa akufuneki lufihle ukungalingani okubanzi, endaweni yolwabiwo ngokutsha okanye ukukhulula amaziko oluntu kwizibophelelo ezisemthethweni.

[/subsection]

[subsection number="08"]

### Amandla okuphendula, ukuchasa, kunye nokuphonononga

Igunya elithunyelweyo kufuneka lihlale libonakala, lilinganiselwe, licaciswe, kwaye lihlaziywa. Amalungu adinga iindlela ezibambekayo zokubuza imibuzo, ukuvakalisa izikhundla zabambalwa, ukucela umngeni kwiinkokeli kwaye afune ukuphononongwa ngokuzimeleyo ngaphandle kokuziphindezela.

[/subsection]

[subsection number="09"]

### Ukuzimela kunye nomanyano

Imibutho kufuneka ilawule iinjongo zayo ngelixa ihlala ikhululekile ukusebenzisana. Amaqumrhu amakhulu kufuneka afumane igunya kwimibutho yovoto ngokugunyazisa okucacileyo endaweni yokuqokelela amandla angeyomfuneko kwiziko.

[/subsection]

[subsection number="10"]

### Uphuhliso oluvulekileyo kunye nophuhliso lwe-reflexive

Akukho ileli yehlabathi jikelele ukusuka kumbutho "wemveli" ukuya "kumbutho wangoku". Imibutho kufuneka iphonononge ukuba izenzo zabo zenza ukuba kwenzeke ntoni na, zinika umdla kabani na, loluphi uqikelelo oluye lwathathwa njengento nje kwaye ngawaphi amava acebisa ukuba atshintshe.

[/subsection]

[/section-layout]


[section-layout label="03 / Isakhelo" surface="shaded"]

## Isakhelo solungiselelo.

Ezi zibophelelo ziguqulela imithetho-siseko ibe yimigaqo-siseko kunye nezenzo zemihla ngemihla amalungu anokuthi aziqonde, azisebenzise, kwaye azihlaziye kunye.

[subsection number="01"]

### Qala ngenjongo ekwabelwana ngayo

Vumelanani ukuba yintoni edibanisa abantu, abachatshazelwa ngumsebenzi, oko amalungu anethemba lokukufeza, iimeko afuna ukuzitshintsha kunye nendlela enokuphuhlisa ngayo injongo.

[/subsection]

[subsection number="02"]

### Yenza umgaqo-siseko ube sisivumelwano sokusebenza

Misa ubulungu, ukwenza izigqibo, amagunya anikezelweyo, izibonelelo, uxanduva, ungquzulwano, ukulungiswa, kunye nokuvalwa kolwimi kunye neefomathi amalungu anokuzisebenzisa. Umgaqo-siseko kufuneka uhlaziywe endaweni yokuba uphathwe njengoxwebhu lokuseka oluthi luthi shwaka lube yifayile. Isicwangciso [seSixhobo sokuYila soMgaqo-siseko soMbutho](/xh/stadgar) esicwangcisiweyo siya kujolisa ekuboneleleni ngedrafti yokuqala ukuze amalungu axoxe, alungelelanise kwaye ammkele; ayikafumaneki. Uphuhliso [ithala leencwadi lephethini yolawulo](/en/governance-patterns) libonelela ngeendlela ezingekavavanywa zophando kunye nophononongo oludibeneyo.

[/subsection]

[subsection number="03"]

### Gcina izigqibo ezibalulekileyo kunye namalungu

Chonga ukuba zeziphi na izigqibo ezifuna ubulungu ngokwawo kwaye zeziphi ezinokuphathiswa. Utshintsho olubalulekileyo kwinjongo, imithetho yomgaqo-siseko, amagunya obunkokeli okanye i-asethi enkulu ekwabelwana ngayo kufuneka ibuyele kumalungu.

[/subsection]

[subsection number="04"]

### Kugunyaziswe ngaphandle kokuthatha indawo yezopolitiko

Nika abameli igunya elaneleyo lokwenza umsebenzi oyimfuneko, kodwa bachaze igunya labo, ixesha, imisebenzi yokunika ingxelo, uphononongo kunye nokususwa. Ugunyaziso kufuneka lulandele ubudlelwane obuqhubekayo:

** igunya → isenzo → ingxelo → hlola → ukuhlaziya**

[/subsection]

[subsection number="05"]

### Yenza ukuthatha inxaxheba kusebenziseke

Yila izaziso, iintlanganiso, amaxwebhu, ukuvota, kunye nonxibelelwano malunga nabantu ekufuneka bazisebenzise. Ukubonelela ngofikelelo kulwimi olufanelekileyo, iifomathi ezifikelelekayo, uthatho-nxaxheba oluxhaswayo kunye neendawo zokuhlala ezifanelekileyo ngaphandle kokuzixela ngokungeyomfuneko ngokobuqu okanye ngonyango.

[/subsection]

[subsection number="06"]

### Ukusasaza ulwazi kunye namandla ombutho

Bhala umsebenzi obalulekileyo, izakhono zokwabelana kunye nokulandelelana kwesicwangciso. Imali, itekhnoloji, ukuququzelela, ubudlelwane bangaphandle, kunye nenkumbulo yombutho akufuneki ukuba zigxile ngokungeyomfuneko kubantu abayimfuneko.

[/subsection]

[subsection number="07"]

### Khusela ukudibana kwedemokhrasi kunye nokuchasana

Dala amathuba azinzileyo abantu kwiiyantlukwano zoluntu ukuba basebenze kunye ngeenjongo ekwabelwana ngazo, ngaphandle kokwenza ngathi ukungalingani kwesakhiwo kuye kwanyamalala. Phatha ukungavisisani kunye nokuqiqa okuncinci njengemithombo yolwazi kunobungqina bokungathembeki.

[/subsection]

[subsection number="08"]

### Umphathi wabelana ngezibonelelo kwaye aziqhelanise nokuncedisana

Vumelanani ngendlela imali, i-asethi, ulwazi, ukhathalelo, izithuba, kunye nezinye izixhobo eziqhelekileyo ezibonelelwa ngayo, zisetyenziswe, zirekhodwe kwaye zidluliselwe. Yahlula ubunzima ekuziphatheni kakubi kwaye uphephe ukukhuphela ngaphandle abantu ngokuzolileyo kuba bengakwazi ukuhlangabezana nesiqhelo. Apho iimfuno zivela kwiimeko ezibanzi zolwakhiwo, buza ukuba ingaba impendulo efanelekileyo ikukuncedana, ukusasazwa ngokutsha, ibango loluntu, okanye indibaniselwano yezi.

[/subsection]

[subsection number="09"]

### Ukuseka uphononongo oluzimeleyo

Abantu abasebenzisa igunya abaliphathisiweyo akufuneki babe noxanduva lokuzihlola ngokwabo. Amalungu kufuneka atyumbe abahlaziyi abazimeleyo abafanelekileyo kwisikali kunye neengozi zombutho.

[/subsection]

[subsection number="10"]

### Gcina iirekhodi ezithembekileyo nezifikelelekayo

Rekhoda izigqibo, uxanduva kunye nolwazi lwemali kwiifom amalungu anokuhlola. Apho kufanelekileyo, amalungu afanele aqinisekise ukuba iirekhodi zentlanganiso zibonisa ngokuchanekileyo oko bekugqitywe.

[/subsection]

[subsection number="11"]

### Lungisa impixano kunye nokwenzakala ngokufanelekileyo

Yahlula ukungavisisani kwihambo eyingozi. Ukubonelela ngeendlela ezifikelelekayo zokuphakamisa iinkxalabo, ezinye iindlela xa abantu abanamandla bebandakanyeka, amathuba okuphendula, amayeza alinganayo kunye nophononongo.

[/subsection]

[subsection number="12"]

### Khusela ukuzimela kombutho

Yenza iimeko eziphambili zenkxaso-mali, intsebenziswano, ubudlelwane bezopolitiko kunye nokuxhomekeka kwamaziko kubonakale. Amalungu kufuneka akwazi ukugweba ukuba inkxaso yangaphandle iqondisa ngokutsha izinto eziphambili, intetho okanye ulawulo.

[/subsection]

[subsection number="13"]

### Funda ngentshukumo kunye nophononongo lwamaxesha ngamaxesha

Sebenzisa izenzo ezidityanisiweyo, iziphumo zayo kunye nokucamngca njengemithombo yolwazi. Ngamathuba athile uvavanya amandla, ubudlelwane bedemokhrasi, ingcamango kunye nokucingelwa okuthatyathiweyo, umlibo wokuzalwa kunye nokungafundi, ukuthatha inxaxheba kunye nokufikelela, emva koko ubuyisele okufundiweyo kwisenzo.

[/subsection]

[subsection number="14"]

### Umanyano ngaphandle kokubekwa kwindawo enye ngokungeyomfuneko

Apho intsebenziswano kumlinganiselo omkhulu iluncedo, cacisa ukuba ngawaphi amagunya ahlala esekhaya kwaye ngawaphi anikezelwe phezulu. Abathunywa kufuneka baphathe izigunyaziso kwaye banike ingxelo.

[/subsection]

[subsection number="15"]

### Sebenzisa umthetho njengomgangatho kunye nesixhobo

Chonga ifomu yomthetho kunye nemigaqo-nkqubo ethile ehambelana nombutho. Hlangana namalungelo asebenzayo, ubhaliso, ingxelo, ukulingana, ubumfihlo, irhafu, ingqesho, imali okanye eminye imisebenzi ngaphandle kokubhidanisa ukuthotyelwa kwezomthetho nomgangatho wedemokhrasi. I [legal matrix](/en/legal-matrix) sisixhobo sophando lwethutyana; iirekhodi zayo zokuqala zilindele ukuqinisekiswa.

[/subsection]

[/section-layout]


[section-layout label="04 / Ngokuziqhelanisa"]

## Ukusebenzisa ukuzilawula.

Imibutho eyahlukeneyo inokusebenzisa amagama ahlukeneyo, iiofisi, kunye neefom zeentlanganiso. Khetha amalungiselelo ahambelana nomsebenzi, ngelixa ugcina igunya liphendula kumalungu.

[subsection number="01"]

### Amalungu

Amalungu enza isiseko sedemokhrasi yombutho. Amalungelo abo, uxanduva, ukwamkelwa, ukunqunyanyiswa, ukuphuma, kunye neendlela zokuthatha inxaxheba kufuneka zicace.

[/subsection]

[subsection number="02"]

### Intlanganiso kawonke-wonke okanye indibano

Iforam ephambili yokwenza izigqibo kufuneka ijongane nemibandela ebekelwe amalungu kuphela, ifumane iingxelo, iphonononge igunya eliphathiswe amagunya ize izihlomele imigaqo yolawulo. Igama elichanekileyo, amaxesha amaninzi kunye nenkqubo inokwahluka.

[/subsection]

[subsection number="03"]

### Ibhodi, ikomiti okanye amalungu agunyazisiweyo

Ibhodi okanye iqumrhu elilinganayo lenza umsebenzi phakathi kweentlanganiso zamalungu ngokwamagunya achaziweyo. Ubukho bayo akufunekanga buguqule uxanduva lolawulo olugunyazisiweyo lube lulawulo phezu kombutho.

[/subsection]

[subsection number="04"]

### Amaqela asebenzayo kunye nabaququzeleli

Imibutho inokudala amaqela okwethutyana okanye aqhubayo kwimisebenzi ethile. Izigunyaziso zabo, izixhobo, kunye nobudlelwane bokunika ingxelo kufuneka bucace, kwaye uququzelelo kufuneka luxhase uthatho-nxaxheba endaweni yokuseka igunya lobungqondi.

[/subsection]

[subsection number="05"]

### Abahlaziyi abazimeleyo

Amalungu atyumba abantu abazimeleyo ngokwaneleyo kwabo balawula umbutho ukuba bahlolisise iimali, izigunyaziso, inkqubo, iingxabano zomdla kunye nezinye izibophelelo eziphathekayo. Imibutho emikhulu okanye elawulwayo inokufuna uphicotho lobuchwephesha ukongeza kuphononongo lwedemokhrasi.

[/subsection]

[subsection number="06"]

### Umgaqo-siseko, iirekhodi kunye nenkumbulo yombutho

Amalungu kufuneka akwazi ukufikelela kwimithetho elawulayo, izigqibo ezibalulekileyo, iinkcukacha zemali, kunye neerekhodi ezifunekayo ukuqonda umbutho. Inkumbulo yombutho kufuneka isinde kwiinguqu zobunkokeli.

[/subsection]

[subsection number="07"]

### Ukuqala umbutho

Uluhlu lokuqalisa lokuxoxa kunye nokuziqhelanisa kunye:

1. Ukuchonga abantu, injongo ekwabelwana ngayo kunye nabantu abachaphazelekayo ngokuphathekayo ngumsebenzi.
2. Xoxa ngobulungu, ukuthatha inxaxheba, ulwimi, ukufikelela kokukhubazeka kunye nemiqobo yempahla phambi kokulungisa iinkqubo.
3. Chonga ifomu yomthetho kunye nemisebenzi ethile enokusebenza.
4. Vumelanani ukuba zeziphi izigqibo ezishiyekileyo kumalungu kwaye zeziphi ezinokuthi zinikezelwe.
5. Idrafti kwaye yamkele ngokudibeneyo umgaqo-siseko onokusetyenziswa.
6. Khetha abameli kunye nabahlaziyi abazimeleyo, bachaze izigunyaziso zabo kunye nokulandelelana kwesicwangciso.
7. Vumelanani ngendlela eza kusingathwa ngayo imali, izinto zexabiso, iirekhodi, izinto zabucala kunye nokungqubana kwemidla.
8. Rekhoda kwaye uqinisekise izigqibo zokuseka kunye nokuqeshwa.
9. Lungiselela ibhanki, ubhaliso, imvume yerhafu okanye iimvume ezilawulwayo apho zifuneka khona.
10. Qala umsebenzi, emva koko uphonononge oko kuveliswa yimigaqo ekusebenzeni kwaye uyilungise xa kuyimfuneko.

[/subsection]

[subsection number="08"]

### Uphononongo oluhlanu oluphindaphindiweyo lwedemokhrasi

Imibutho inokusebenzisa amaxesha ngamaxesha iindlela ezintlanu zophononongo olufutshane:

**Ulawulo lwegunya kunye nokubanakho:** Kuphi ugxininiso lwamandla, kwaye ngawaphi amandla amatsha okanye amathuba avelayo?

** ubudlelwane beDemokhrasi:** Ngubani othatha inxaxheba, ngubani ongavumelaniyo, amaqela ahlukeneyo anxulumana njani, kwaye yimvisiswano efihla ukungalingani?

**I-Ideology kunye ne-doxa:** Umbutho uvuza ntoni, uhlengahlengise okanye wenze kubonakale kungenakwenzeka, kubandakanywa nentelekelelo ekungekho mntu ukhe wacinga ukuyibuza?

** Umnombo kunye nokungafundi:** Zivela phi iindidi kunye neendlela zethu zesiqhelo, kwaye ngaba siyabhidanisa ubuchwephesha, isikali okanye isiko esililifa kunye nemfuneko okanye inkqubela?

**Uthatho-nxaxheba kunye nofikelelo:** Ngubani ongakwaziyo ukusebenzisa iinkqubo eziqhelekileyo, yeyiphi imiqobo evelisa oko kukhutshelwa ecaleni, kwaye loluphi utshintsho olufunekayo?

Olu phononongo kufuneka lukhokelele kwizigqibo, kuxanduva olwabelweyo kwaye, apho kufanelekileyo, inyathelo elidityanelweyo ngaphaya kombutho kunokuba libe ngumfuziselo wokubonisa.

[/subsection]

### Ukuphendula

Umsebenzi wokucacisa izigqibo, ukuphendula kwabo bachaphazelekayo, ukwamkela ukuphononongwa nokulungisa iintsilelo apho kuyimfuneko.

[/glossary-entry]

[glossary-entry]

### Umbutho

Abantu abazilungiselela ngokuzithandela malunga nenjongo ekwabelwana ngayo kwaye bayavuma indlela yokulawula umsebenzi wabo odibeneyo.

[/glossary-entry]

[glossary-entry]

### Ukusebenza ngokubambisana

Inkolelo yeqela elinamava, esekelwe ekusebenzeni, yokuba lingenza kunye kwaye lichaphazele iimeko zalo.

[/glossary-entry]

[glossary-entry]

### Intsebenziswano

Abantu badibanisa ulwazi olwahlukileyo, amandla, kunye nezibonelelo kwisenzo ekwabelwana ngaso ngelixa begcina isakhono sokungavumelani, ukukhuphisana namandla kunye nokucela umngeni kwiimeko abasebenza phantsi kwazo.

[/glossary-entry]

[glossary-entry]

### Commons

Izibonelelo, ulwazi, iziseko zophuhliso okanye amandla abanjwe okanye alawulwa ngeenjongo ekwabelwana ngazo phantsi kwemigaqo eyenziweyo okanye eyamkelwa ngabantu abachaphazelekayo.

[/glossary-entry]

[glossary-entry]

### Ukuhambelana

Ubudlelwane apho iyantlukwano inegalelo kwisakhono ekwabelwana ngaso ngaphandle kokubekwa kwizikhundla eziphezulu nezingaphantsi.

[/glossary-entry]

[glossary-entry]

### Umgaqo-siseko

Imithetho yolawulo ekuvunyelwene ngayo yombutho malunga nenjongo, ubulungu, igunya, ukwenza izigqibo, izixhobo, ukuphendula, ukulungiswa kunye nokuvalwa.

[/glossary-entry]

[glossary-entry]

### Abathunywa

Ukunika umntu okanye iqumrhu igunya elisikelwe umda ukuba lisebenze egameni lombutho ngaphandle kokudlulisela igunya lokugqibela lobulungu.

[/glossary-entry]

[glossary-entry]

### Uphuhliso lwedemokhrasi

Uphuhliso oluvulelekileyo lweemeko kunye namandla adibeneyo apho abantu banokuqonda, balawule, kwaye baguqule ubomi babo obufanayo, ngaphandle kokuthatha indlela yehlabathi jikelele yenkqubela phambili.

[/glossary-entry]

[glossary-entry]

### Ukuphikisa

Ukuvakalisa ukungavumelani nesigqibo esicetywayo okanye esele sikhona, utoliko okanye isenzo. Kwi-DDC ithathwa njengomthombo wolwazi kunokuba ungathembeki.

[/glossary-entry]

[glossary-entry]

### Ulawulo

Ubudlelwane obuhlala ixesha elide apho abanye abantu okanye amaziko asebenzisa amandla angaphendulekiyo okanye ngokwesakhiwo awongamileyo kunabanye.

[/glossary-entry]

[glossary-entry]

### Doxa

Iintelekelelo kunye nezenzo ezibonakala ziyindalo okanye zicacile kangangokuba zidla ngokuveliswa ngaphandle kokuba kuxoxwe ngazo.

[/glossary-entry]

[glossary-entry]

### Federation

Intsebenziswano phakathi kwemibutho ezimeleyo ngombutho ekwabelwana ngawo amagunya awo agunyaziswe ngokucacileyo yimibutho yawo.

[/glossary-entry]

[glossary-entry]

### Inkululeko yokunxulumana

Inkululeko yokwenza, ukujoyina, ukuxhasa, okanye ukushiya imibutho kunye nokusebenza ngokubambisana nabanye, ngokuxhomekeke kumalungelo omgaqo-siseko kunye nomthetho osebenzayo.

[/glossary-entry]

[glossary-entry]

### Uluhlu lwemigangatho

Ubudlelwane obulungelelanisiweyo bokuphakama kunye nokuthotyelwa. Umahluko kwindima okanye kubuchwephesha asikokuhlela ngokuzenzekelayo; umba wendlela amandla akhiwe ngayo.

[/glossary-entry]

[glossary-entry]

### I-Ideology ekusebenzeni

Iindlela amaziko kunye neendlela eziqhubekayo zokunxibelelana ngento ekhoyo, into exatyisiweyo, into enokwenzeka kunye neentlobo zeendima zentlalo ekulindeleke ukuba abantu bazithathe.

[/glossary-entry]

[glossary-entry]

### Uphononongo oluzimeleyo

Uphononongo olwenziwa ngabantu abazimeleyo ngokwaneleyo kwabo bazigqibo zabo, iimali okanye ulawulo lwabo luphononongwayo.

[/glossary-entry]

[glossary-entry]

### Ugunyaziso

Injongo, igunya kunye nemida enikwe ummeli, umthunywa, ikomiti okanye igosa.

[/glossary-entry]

[glossary-entry]

### Ulongamo lwelungu

Umthetho-siseko wokuba amagunya aphezulu kumbutho axhomekeke kumalungu awo endaweni yebhodi yawo, abasebenzi, abaxhasi ngezimali okanye iingcaphephe zangaphandle.

[/glossary-entry]

[glossary-entry]

### Ukuncedisana

Umbutho ohambelanayo apho abantu badibanisa amandla okanye izixhobo zokuhlangabezana neemfuno ekwabelwana ngazo, endaweni yesisa esinecala elinye ukusuka kubancedisi ukuya kubaxhamli.

[/glossary-entry]

[glossary-entry]

### Iprefiguration

Ukuziqhelanisa kumaziko angoku ubudlelwane bedemokhrasi kunye neendlela zentsebenziswano abantu abafuna ukuzenza ngokubanzi ukuba kwenzeke.

[/glossary-entry]

[glossary-entry]

### Praksis

Ubudlelwane obuphindaphindiweyo phakathi kwesenzo kunye nokucamngca apho abantu batolika iimeko, benze, bafunde kwiziphumo kwaye bahlaziye oko bakwenzayo.

[/glossary-entry]

[glossary-entry]

### Indawo yokuhlala efanelekileyo

Uhlengahlengiso oluyimfuneko nolufanelekileyo oluvumela umntu okhubazekileyo ukuba athathe inxaxheba ngokulinganayo apho iindlela zokufikeleleka ngokubanzi zingonelanga, ngaphandle kokubeka umthwalo ongafanelekanga.

[/glossary-entry]

[glossary-entry]

### Ulwabiwo ngokutsha

Utshintsho lwangabom kwindlela izinto eziphathekayo, amathuba, iinzuzo okanye imithwalo esabiwa ngayo, nokuba kuphakathi kombutho okanye ngamalungiselelo abanzi oluntu kunye noluntu.

[/glossary-entry]

[glossary-entry]

### Injongo ekwabelwana ngayo

Injongo efanayo enika umbutho isizathu sokubakho kwaye ukhokele ukusetyenziswa kwezibonelelo.

[/glossary-entry]

[glossary-entry]

### Uthatho-nxaxheba oluxhaswayo

Ukuthatha inxaxheba apho umntu asebenzisa uncedo, ukutolika, inkxaso yonxibelelwano, okanye olunye uncedo ngelixa egcina i-arhente yakhe kunye nokuma kwezopolitiko.

[/glossary-entry]

[glossary-entry]

### Uyilo lwendalo yonke

Ukuyila imo engqongileyo, ulwazi, iinkqubo kunye nezixhobo ukuze zisetyenziswe lolona luhlu lubanzi lwabantu ukusuka ekuqaleni kunokuthembela kuphela kuhlengahlengiso lwamva lomntu.

[/glossary-entry]

[glossary-entry]

### Umbutho wokuzithandela

Umbutho owenziwe ngesivumelwano phakathi kwabantu endaweni yokuba ubulungu obusisinyanzelo okanye ukudalwa kwangaphambili ngurhulumente.

[/glossary-entry]
