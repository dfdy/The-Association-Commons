## Ukuphila ingomuso nje

Ipolitiki yokuphila ingomuso nje itjho ukwenza ubudlelwano neendlela zokuhlela esifuna ukuzibona emphakathini ngendlela esisebenzisana ngayo namhlanjesi. Iindlela zitjengisa iminqopho: ukuzibandakanya ngokuzithandela, ukuthatha iinqunto ngokuhlanganyela nokuphendulana ngezenzo zethu.

I-The Democratic Development Commons inikela ihlaka lokuzihlela ngokuzithandela abantu abangalisebenzisa nje, ngokufunda ngokuhlanganyela, ukusizana, nokuzibusa ngokwentando yenengi. Amalunga angacocisana, atjhugulule, begodu athuthukise iinkambiso zalo emithethwenisisekelo yawo nasekuzihleleni kwamalanga woke, athathe imithethosisekelo yawo njengeemvumelwano eziphilako ezenza ukuzibandakanya kukghonakale begodu zitjhuguluke ngokuhlangenwe nakho okwabelwanako. Ithulusi elihleliweko lokwakha imithethosisekelo nebuthelelo leendlela zokuphatha elisathuthukiswako kuhloswe ngakho ukusekela ikambiso le, kukulapho amalunga asagcine amandla phezu kwemithetho ayemukelako. Ihlakeli alisiwo umthetho ophakanyiswako, begodu ukulamukela akujamiseleli imithetho ekhona.

## Ukuhlanganyela kuthoma ebantwini.

Abantu kufanele bakhululeke ukuzibandakanya ngeentlhogo, iinhloso, namathuba afanako, begodu balawule iinkhungo abazidalako. Ukusebenzisana kutjho ukwenza ndawonye hlangana nokungafani; akutlhogi ukuzwana nofana ukukhipha ukungavumelani, ukuphikisana nofana ukutjhijilela okuhlelekileko kwamandla.

I-ejensi yentando yenengi ayinikelwa abazuzi abangasebenziko ziingcwethi, iinhlangano, iimakethe nofana umbuso. Ithuthuka ngamandla wabantu wokuzwisisa ubujamo babo, ukwenza iinqunto, ukutlhogomela omunye nomunye, ukuphatha iinsetjenziswa ezabelwana ngazo, ukutjhijilela ukubusa, ukufunda ngezenzo nokubuyekeza amalungiselelo abaphila ngawo begodu basebenze ndawonye. Iinkhungo zomphakathi nanyana kunjalo zigcina izibopho zazo zokunikela amalungelo, izinto zomphakathi kanye nemisebenzi yokuziphendulela.

I-Democratic Development Commons inikela ngohlaka olwabelwanako lokwenza lokhu. Ithatha amasiko ahlukahlukeneko weSewula Afrika womsebenzi, womphakathi, wokusizana, wefundo, wokukholwa, womfundi, womakhelwana nezinye iinhlangano zokuzithandela; ngemikhuba ewusizo eyakhiwe kwenye indawo; kanye nokuzibophelela komthethosisekelo kwekululeko, ukulingana, isithunzi, nokuzibandakanya.

Akubeki ifomu elilodwa lehlangano nofana indlela yokuthuthukisa. Iinhlangano ezihlukahlukeneko zingagcina iinhloso zazo, amabizo, amasiko, nezakhiwo ngesikhathi zibuza imibuzo ejayelekileko yentando yenengi ngamandla, ukuzibandakanya, ilwazi, iinsetjenziswa, ukuziphendulela nokutjhuguluka.

Iinhlangano zihlala zinomthwalo wokukhomba indlela yomthetho nemisebenzi ekhethekileko yomsebenzi efanele umsebenzi wazo.

## Iinkambisolawulo ezilitjhumi zokuhlela.

Iinkambisolawulo zokuhlela zivulekele ukukhulumisana, ukuhlolwa, nokulungiswa. Banikela ngemibuzo ehlanganyelweko yemihlobo ehlukileko yokuhlobana, kunokobana kube yimodeli eyodwa yehlangano.

[subsection number="01"]

### Ikululeko nokuhlanganyela ngokuzithandela

Abantu bangakha, bajoyine, basekele, nofana batjhiye iinhlangano ngaphandle kokulawulwa kwangaphandle okungatlhogekiko. Okulindelwe malunga, imithwalo yemfanelo nemiphumela kufanele izwisiseke ngaphambi kobana abantu bazibophelele.

[/subsection]

[subsection number="02"]

### Ukuma okulinganako

Ilunga ngalinye lithathwa njengomuntu okghona ukucabanga, ukwahlulela nokuzibandakanya. Umehluko elwazini, ikghono, isipiliyoni, ubujamo, nofana umthwalo awenzi amalunga aphezulu naphasi.

[/subsection]

[subsection number="03"]

### Ukuzibusa ngokuhlanganyela

Amalunga agcina igunya lokugcina phezu kwehloso yenhlangano, umthethosisekelo, kanye nemithombo emikhulu ehlanganyelweko. Abajameli neengcwethi basebenzisa amandla anikelwa wona kunokobana bathathe isikhundla se-ejensi yamalunga.

[/subsection]

[subsection number="04"]

### Umehluko ngaphandle kokubusa

Intando yenengi ayitlhogi ukufana. Umehluko kufanele ukhulise amandla wokuhlanganyela kunokobana uqinise ubudlelwano bokuyala, ukuthembela, ukungafakwa namkha ukuphakama.

[/subsection]

[subsection number="05"]

### Ukuzibandakanya okukhulu nokufikeleleka

Ilungelo elihlelekileko lokuzibandakanya alikaneli lapho ilimi, ukufunda nokutlola, ukukhubazeka, ukuthutha, iindleko, ukutlhogonyelwa, umsebenzi, ukuphepha, ithekhnoloji, nofana ubujamo bezenhlalakahle kwenza ukuzibandakanya kungasetjenziswa. Ihlangano yentando yenengi kufanele isuse iinqabo ngokusebenzisa umklamo jikelele, indawo yokuhlala ezwakalako, kanye neendlela ezinengi zokuzibandakanya.

[/subsection]

[subsection number="06"]

### Ukufunda ngokuhlanganyela nokubuza

Ukuhleleka kuyafundisa. Amalunga afunda ngemihlangano, imikhankaso, irhubhululo, ukungqubuzana, ukubeka imali, ukutlhogomela, umsebenzi osebenzako, nokuzindla. Ubungcweti kufanele bukhulise ukuzwisisa okuhlangeneko kunokobana kusungulwe ubukhosi phezu kwelwazi.

[/subsection]

[subsection number="07"]

### Ukuphatha okuhlanganyelweko, ukusizana, kanye nomthwalo wemfanelo wemvelo

Imali, ilwazi, iindawo, iinsetjenziswa, isikhathi, ukutlhogomela, nezinye iinsetjenziswa ezabelwana ngazo kufanele zisebenzele iinhloso ezivunyelweko kunokunothisa ngasese. Iinhlangano kufanele zihlele ukusekela lapho kufanele khona begodu zicabangele imiphumela yemvelo yemisebenzi yazo. Ukusizana kungenza bona ukuhlanganyela nokubumbana kube khona, kodwana akukafaneli kufihle ukungalingani okukhulu, kuthathe isikhundla sokusabalalisa kabutjha namkha kukhulule iinkhungo zomphakathi eenbopheni ezisemthethweni.

[/subsection]

[subsection number="08"]

### Amandla wokuziphendulela, ukuphikisana, nokubuyekeza

Igunya elinikelweko kufanele lihlale libonakala, lilinganiselwe, lichazeke, begodu libuyekezwe. Amalunga atlhoga iindlela ezisebenzako zokubuza iinqunto, ukuveza ubujamo obuncani, ukutjhijilela abadosiphambili nokufuna ukuhlolisisa okuzijameleko ngaphandle kokuziphindiselela.

[/subsection]

[subsection number="09"]

### Ukuzijamela nokuhlangana

Iinhlangano kufanele zilawule iinhloso zazo lokha zihlala zitjhaphulukile ukusebenzisana. Izakhiwo ezinkulu kufanele zithole igunya eenhlanganweni ezibunjwako ngokusebenzisa ukuthunyelwa okucacileko kunokobana zibuthelele amandla angatlhogekiko esikhungweni.

[/subsection]

[subsection number="10"]

### Ukuthuthukiswa okuvulekileko nokucabangako

Alikho ilede elisuka "kwesiko" ukuya "kwesimanjemanje". Iinhlangano kufanele zihlolisise bona yini imikghwa yazo eyenza bona yenzeke, iinrhuluphelo zabani ezisebenzela, ngimiphi imicabango esele ithathwa njengento elula nokuthi ngikuphi okuhlangenwe nakho okuveza bona kufanele kutjhuguluke.

[/subsection]

[/section-layout]


[section-layout label="03 / Ihlaka" surface="shaded"]

## Ihlaka lokuhlela.

Izibopho lezi zitjhugulula iinkambisolawulo zibe mthethosisekelo nemikhuba yangelanga amalunga angayizwisisa, ayisebenzise, begodu ayibuyekeze ndawonye.

[subsection number="01"]

### Thoma ngenhloso ehlanganyelweko

Vumelana ngokuthi khuyini ehlanganisa abantu, ngubani othintwa msebenzi, khuyini amalunga athemba ukuyifeza, ngiziphi iimeko abafuna ukuzitjhugulula nokuthi umnqopho ungathuthuka njani.

[/subsection]

[subsection number="02"]

### Yenza umthethosisekelo ube sivumelwano sokusebenza

Beka ubulunga, ukwenza iinqunto, amandla anikelwe, iinsetjenziswa, ukuziphendulela, ukungqubuzana, ukutjhugululwa, nokuvala ngelimi namafomethi amalunga angasebenzisa. Umthethosisekelo kufanele ubuyekezwe kunokuthi uphathwe njengedokhumenti yokusungula enyamalala efayilini. [Ithulusi lokuTlola umThethosisekelo weNhlangano](/nr/stadgar) elihleliweko lizakuhlose ukunikela ngedrafti yokuthoma yokuthi amalunga akhulume, atjhugulule, begodu amukele; ayikatholakali. I-[governance-phettern library](/en/governance-patterns) ethuthukako inikela ngeenkhetho ezingakahlolwa zokwenza irhubhululo nokubuyekezwa okuhlangeneko.

[/subsection]

[subsection number="03"]

### Gcina iinqunto eziqakathekileko namalunga

Hlola bona ngiziphi iinqunto ezitlhoga ubulungu ngokwabo nokuthi ngiziphi ezingadluliselwa kwabanye. Amatjhuguluko aqakathekileko ehlosweni, imithetho yomthethosisekelo, amandla woburholi nofana ipahla ekulu ehlanganyelweko kufanele ibuyele emalungwini.

[/subsection]

[subsection number="04"]

### Thumela isithunywa ngaphandle kokuthatha isikhundla sezepolotiki

Nikela abajameli igunya elaneleko lokwenza umsebenzi otlhogekako, kodwana hlathulula igunya labo, isikhathi, imisebenzi yokubika, ukubuyekeza, nokususwa. Ukuthunyelwa kufanele kulandele ubudlelwano obubuyelelweko:

**igunya → isenzo → umbiko → ukuhlolisisa → ukubuyekeza**

[/subsection]

[subsection number="05"]

### Yenza ukuzibandakanya kube lula

Yenza izaziso, imihlangano, amadokhumende, ukuvowuda, nokukhulumisana ebantwini okufanele bazisebenzise. Nikela ngokufikelela ilimi elifaneleko, amafomethi afinyelelekako, ukuzibandakanya okusekelweko kanye nendawo yokuhlala ezwakalako ngaphandle kokuvezwa okungakafaneli komuntu mathupha nofana kwezokwelapha.

[/subsection]

[subsection number="06"]

### Sabalalisa ilwazi namandla wehlangano

Tlola umsebenzi oqakathekileko, wabelane ngamakghono begodu uhlele ukulandelana. Imali, ithekhnoloji, ukusiza, ubudlelwano bangaphandle, kanye nenkumbulo yenhlangano akukafaneli bona igxile khulu ebantwini abaqakathekileko.

[/subsection]

[subsection number="07"]

### Vikela ukuhlangana kwentando yenengi nokuphikisana

Yenza amathuba ahlalako wokuthi abantu abahlukahlukeneko bezenhlalakahle basebenze ndawonye ngeenhloso ezifanako, ngaphandle kokwenza kwangathi ukungalingani kwesakhiwo sekuphelile. Phatha ukungavumelani nokucabanga kwabantu abancani njengemithombo yolwazi kunobufakazi bokungathembeki.

[/subsection]

[subsection number="08"]

### Umphathi wabelane ngeensetjenziswa begodu ukusizana ngokuzijayeza

Vumelana ngokuthi imali, ipahla, ilwazi, ukutlhogonyelwa, iindawo, nezinye iinsetjenziswa ezijayelekileko zinikelwa njani, zisetjenziswa njani, zirekhodwa begodu zidluliselwa njani. Hlukanisa ubudisi nokuziphatha kumbi begodu ukhandele ukukhipha abantu ngokuthula ngombana abakghoni ukuhlangabezana nomthetho othathwako. Lapho iindingo zivela khona ngebanga lobujamo obubanzi besakhiwo, buza bona ipendulo efaneleko kusizana, ukusabalalisa kabutjha, isibawo somphakathi, nofana enye inhlanganisela yalokhu.

[/subsection]

[subsection number="09"]

### Yenza ukuhlolisisa okuzijameleko

Abantu abasebenzisa igunya abanikelweko akukafaneli babe nesibopho sokuzibuyekeza bona. Amalunga kufanele akhethe ababuyekezi abazijameleko abafanele ubukhulu nobungozi benhlangano.

[/subsection]

[subsection number="10"]

### Gcina amarekhodi athembekileko begodu afinyelelekako

Tlola iinqunto, imithwalo yemfanelo kanye nelwazi lezezimali emafomini amalunga angazihlola. Lapho kufanele khona, amalunga kufanele aqinisekise bona amarekhodi womhlangano atjengisa ngokunembileko lokho okunqunyiweko.

[/subsection]

[subsection number="11"]

### Lungisa ukungezwani nokulimala ngokufaneleko

Hlukanisa ukungavumelani nokuziphatha okulimazako. Nikela ngeendlela ezifikelelekako zokuphakamisa ukutshwenyeka, ezinye iindlela lokha abantu abanamandla nabathintekako, amathuba wokuphendula, amakhambi alinganako nokubuyekeza.

[/subsection]

[subsection number="12"]

### Vikela ukuzijamela kwehlangano

Yenza ubujamo obukhulu bokusekela ngeemali, ukusebenzisana, ubudlelwano bezepolotiki kanye nokuthembela kweenkhungo kubonakale. Amalunga kufanele akghone ukwahlulela bona isekelo langaphandle liqondisa kabutjha izinto eziqakathekileko, ikulumo nofana ukubusa.

[/subsection]

[subsection number="13"]

### Funda ngezenzo nokubuyekeza ngeenkhathi ezithileko

Sebenzisa isenzo esihlangeneko, imiphumela yaso nokuzindla njengemithombo yolwazi. Hlola amandla, ubudlelwano bentando yenengi, imibono kanye nokucabanga okuthathwa njengento elula, umlando wozalo nokungafundi, ukuzibandakanya nokufinyelela, bese ubuyisela lokho okufundileko ekwenzeni.

[/subsection]

[subsection number="14"]

### Ukuhlangana ngaphandle kokuhlanganiswa okungakafaneli

Lapho ukusebenzisana ngezinga elikhulu kuwusizo, tjho bona ngimaphi amandla aseleko wendawo begodu ngimaphi adluliselwa phezulu. Iinthunywa kufanele zibe namagunya begodu zibike.

[/subsection]

[subsection number="15"]

### Sebenzisa umthetho njengesisekelo nesisetjenziswa

Hlola indlela yomthetho nemithetho ekhethekileko yomsebenzi efanele ihlangano. Ukuhlangabezana namalungelo asebenzako, ukutloliswa, ukubika, ukulingana, ubumfihlo, umthelo, umsebenzi, wezeemali nofana eminye imisebenzi ngaphandle kokuphambanisa ukuthobela umthetho nekhwalithi yentando yenengi. I-[legal matrix](/en/legal-matrix) mthombo wokurhubhulula wesikhatjhana; amarekhodi wayo wokuthoma alindele ukuqinisekiswa.

[/subsection]

[/section-layout]


[section-layout label="04 / Ngokwenza"]

## Ukusebenzisa ukuzibusa.

Iinhlangano ezihlukileko zingasebenzisa amagama ahlukileko, amahhovisi, namafomu womhlangano. Khetha amalungiselelo alingana nomsebenzi, ngesikhathi ugcina igunya liziphendulela emalungwini.

[subsection number="01"]

### Amalunga

Amalunga akha isisekelo sentando yenengi senhlangano. Amalungelo wabo, imithwalo yabo, ukwamukelwa, ukumiswa, ukuphuma, kanye neendlela zokuzibandakanya kufanele kucace.

[/subsection]

[subsection number="02"]

### Umhlangano omkhulu nofana umhlangano

Iforamu ekulu yokwenza iinqunto yobulungu kufanele isebenze ngeendaba ezibekelwe amalunga, yamukele imibiko, ibuyekeze igunya elinikelweko begodu itjhugulule imithetho ebusako. Ibizo eliqondileko, imvamisa kanye nekambiso kungahluka.

[/subsection]

[subsection number="03"]

### Ibhodo, ikomidi nofana abaphathi be-ofisi abathunyelwe

Ibhodo nofana umzimba ofanako wenza umsebenzi hlangana nemihlangano yamalunga ngaphakathi kwamandla achaziweko. Ukubakhona kwayo akukafaneli kutjhugulule umthwalo wesigungu esiphezulu ube yibukhosi phezu kwenhlangano.

[/subsection]

[subsection number="04"]

### Amaqembu wokusebenza nabagqugquzeli

Iinhlangano zingakha amaqembu wesikhatjhana nofana aragela phambili wemisebenzi ethileko. Iimvumelwano zabo, iinsetjenziswa, kanye nobudlelwano bokubika kufanele bucace, begodu ukusiza kufanele kusekele ukuzibandakanya kunokusungula igunya lobuhlakani.

[/subsection]

[subsection number="05"]

### Ababuyekezi abazijameleko

Amalunga akhetha abantu abazijameleko ngokwaneleko kilabo abalawula ihlangano ukobana babuyekeze iimali, igunya, ikambiso, ukutjhayisana kwemibono nezinye iimbopho eziphathekako. Iinhlangano ezinkulu nofana ezilawulwako zingatlhoga ukuhlolisiswa ngobungcweti ngaphezu kokubuyekezwa kwentando yenengi.

[/subsection]

[subsection number="06"]

### Umthethosisekelo, amarekhodi kanye nenkumbulo yenhlangano

Amalunga kufanele akghone ukufikelela imithetho ebusako, iinqunto eziqakathekileko, ilwazi lezezimali, namarekhodi atlhogekako ukuzwisisa ihlangano. Inkumbulo yenhlangano kufanele iphile ematjhugulukweni woburholi.

[/subsection]

[subsection number="07"]

### Ukuthoma ihlangano

Irhelo lokuhlola lokuthoma lokucoca nokuvumelanisa ndawonye:

1. Hlola abantu, umnqopho ofanako nabantu abathintekako ngomsebenzi.
2. Xoxa ngobulungu, ukuzibandakanya, ilimi, ukufinyelela kokukhubazeka kanye neenqabo eziphathekako ngaphambi kokulungisa iinkambiso.
3. Khomba indlela yomthetho nemisebenzi ethileko yomsebenzi engasebenza.
4. Vumelana ngokuthi ngiziphi iinqunto eziseleko emalungwini nokuthi ngiziphi ezingathunyelwa.
5. Tlola begodu wamukele ngokuhlanganyela umthethosisekelo ongasetjenziswa.
6. Khetha abajameli nababuyekezi abazijameleko, hlathulula imisebenzi yabo begodu uhlele ukulandelana.
7. Vumelana ngokuthi imali, ipahla, amarekhodi, ubumfihlo, nokungqubuzana kwemibono kuzokuphathwa njani.
8. Rekhoda begodu uqinisekise iinqunto zokusungula nokuqatjhwa.
9. Hlela ukubhadela, ukutlolisa, ukuvunywa komthelo nofana iimvumo ezilawulwako lapho kutlhogeka khona.
10. Thoma umsebenzi, bese ubuyekeza bona imithetho ikhiqiza ini emsebenzini begodu uyitjhugulule lokha nakutlhogekako.

[/subsection]

[subsection number="08"]

### Ukubuyekezwa kwentando yenengi okubuyelelweko okuhlanu

Iinhlangano zingasebenzisa ngeenkhathi ezithileko iindlela ezihlanu zokubuyekeza ezimfitjhani:

**Ukulandelana kwezinto namandla:** Amandla agxila kuphi, begodu ngimaphi amandla amatjha nofana amathuba avelako?

**Ubudlelwano bentando yenengi:** Ngubani ohlanganyelako, ngubani ongavumelani, amaqembu ahlukileko ahlobana njani, begodu ingabe ukuzwana kufihla ukungalingani?

**Imibono ne-doxa:** Khuyini ihlangano evuzako, ekwenza bona kube yinto ejayelekileko namkha eyenza bona kubonakale kungakghonakali, kufaka hlangana ukucabanga okungekho muntu ocabanga ukukubuza?

**Isizukulwana nokungafundi:** Iinkoro zethu nemikghwa yethu zivelaphi, begodu sididanisa na ukwenza umsebenzi, isikali nofana isiko elizuzwe njengelifa nesidingo nofana ituthuko?

**Ukubamba iqhaza nokufinyelela:** Ngubani ongeke akwazi ukusebenzisa iindlela ezijayelekileko, ngiziphi iinqabo ezikhiqiza ukungafakwa, begodu ngiziphi amatjhuguluko atlhogekako?

Ukubuyekezwa lokhu kufanele kurholele ekutheni kube neenqunto, imithwalo eyabelwe begodu, lapho kufanele khona, isenzo esihlangeneko ngaphandle kwenhlangano kunokobana kube ziindlela zokucabangisisa.

[/subsection]

[/glossary-entry]

### Ihlangano

Abantu abazihlela ngokuzithandela ngehloso efanako begodu bavumelana ngokuthi umsebenzi wabo uzokubusa njani.

[/glossary-entry]

[glossary-entry]

### Ukusebenza ngokuhlanganyela

Inkolelo yesiqhema, esekelwe emsebenzini, yokuthi ingasebenza ndawonye begodu ithinte ubujamo bayo.

[/glossary-entry]

[glossary-entry]

### Ukusebenzisana

Abantu abahlanganisa ilwazi elihlukileko, amandla, kanye nemithombo ekwenzeni ngokuhlanganyela ngesikhathi begcina ikghono lokuphikisana, ukuphikisana namandla nokuphikisa ubujamo abasebenza ngabo ndawonye.

[/glossary-entry]

[glossary-entry]

### Iindawo ezijayelekileko

Iinsetjenziswa, ilwazi, umthangalasiseko namkha amandla aphethwe namkha alawulwa ngeenhloso ezihlanganyelweko ngaphasi kwemithetho eyenziwe namkha eyamukelwa babantu abathintekako.

[/glossary-entry]

[glossary-entry]

### Ukuhambisana

Ubudlelwano lapho umehluko ufaka isandla emandleni ahlanganyelweko ngaphandle kokubekwa eendaweni eziphezulu neziphasi.

[/glossary-entry]

[glossary-entry]

### UmThethosisekelo

Imithetho yokulawula evunyelweko yenhlangano malungana nomnqopho, ubulunga, igunya, ukwenza iinqunto, iinsetjenziswa, ukuziphendulela, ukutjhugululwa, nokuvalwa.

[/glossary-entry]

[glossary-entry]

### Ukuthunyelwa

Ukunikela umuntu nofana umzimba igunya elilinganiselweko lokwenza egameni lenhlangano ngaphandle kokudlulisa igunya lokugcina lobulungu.

[/glossary-entry]

[glossary-entry]

### Ukuthuthukiswa kwentando yenengi

Ukuthuthukiswa okuvulekileko kwemibandela namandla ahlangeneko lapho abantu bangazwisisa khona, babuse, begodu batjhugulule ipilo yabo ejayelekileko, ngaphandle kokuthatha indlela yentuthuko yendawo yoke.

[/glossary-entry]

[glossary-entry]

### Ukuphikisana

Ukungavumelani okuvezweko nesiqunto esiphakanyisiweko nofana esikhona, ukuhlathulula nofana umkhuba. Ku-DDC ithathwa njengomthombo wolwazi ongaba khona kunokungathembeki.

[/glossary-entry]

[glossary-entry]

### Ukubusa

Ubudlelwano obuqinileko lapho abanye abantu nofana iinkhungo zisebenzisa amandla angaphezu kwabanye.

[/glossary-entry]

[glossary-entry]

### I-Doxa

Imicabango nemikghwa ebonakala njengemvelo namkha ebonakalako kangangobana ivamise ukukhiqizwa ngaphandle kokuphikiswa.

[/glossary-entry]

[glossary-entry]

### Ifedereshini

Ukusebenzisana hlangana neenhlangano ezizijameleko ngokusebenzisa umzimba owabelwana ngawo amandla wawo anikelwa ngokukhanyako ziinhlangano zawo.

[/glossary-entry]

[glossary-entry]

### Ikululeko yokuzibandakanya

Ikululeko yokwakha, ukujoyina, ukusekela, nofana ukutjhiya iinhlangano nokwenza ngokubambisana nabanye, ngokuya ngamalungelo womthethosisekelo nomthetho osebenzako.

[/glossary-entry]

[glossary-entry]

### Ukulandelana kwezinto

Ubudlelwano obuhlelekileko bokuphakama nokuzithoba. Umehluko wendima nofana ubuchwepheshe akusikho ngokuzenzakalelako; indaba kukuthi amandla ahlelwe njani.

[/glossary-entry]

[glossary-entry]

### Imibono esebenzako

Iindlela iinkhungo nemikhuba eragela phambili nokuthintana ngalokho okukhona, okuqakathekiswako, okungenzeka nokuthi ngimiphi imihlobo yeendima emphakathini abantu abalindeleke bona bazithathe.

[/glossary-entry]

[glossary-entry]

### Ukubuyekezwa okuzijameleko

Ukuhlolisisa okwenziwa babantu abazijameleko ngokwaneleko kilabo abahlolwa iinqunto zabo, imali namkha ukuphatha.

[/glossary-entry]

[glossary-entry]

### Igunya

Umnqopho, igunya nemikhawulo enikelwe umjameli, isithunywa, ikomidi nofana umphathi we-ofisi.

[/glossary-entry]

[glossary-entry]

### Ukuzibusa kwamalunga

Umthetho wokuthi igunya lokugcina phezu kwenhlangano lisemalungwini wayo kunokobana kube yibhodi yayo, abasebenzi, abaxhasi namkha abososayensi bangaphandle.

[/glossary-entry]

[glossary-entry]

### Ukusizana

Ihlangano yokubuyisana lapho abantu bahlanganisa khona amandla nofana iinsetjenziswa ukuhlangabezana neendingo ezifanako, kunokobana kube nesizo eliqondileko ukusuka kubasizi ukuya kubazuzi.

[/glossary-entry]

[glossary-entry]

### Ukuhlela ngaphambilini

Ukuzijayeza eenkhungweni zamanje ubudlelwano bentando yenengi kanye namafomu wokusebenzisana abantu abafuna ukwenza bona kube lula.

[/glossary-entry]

[glossary-entry]

### I-Praxis

Ubudlelwano obubuyelelweko hlangana nesenzo nokuzindla lapho abantu bahlathulula khona ubujamo, benze, bafunde emiphumeleni begodu babuyekeze lokho abakwenzako.

[/glossary-entry]

[glossary-entry]

### Indawo yokuhlala ezwakalako

Ukulungiswa okutlhogekako nokufaneleko okwenza bona umuntu okhubazekileko akghone ukuzibandakanya ngokulinganako lapho amagadango wokufikeleleka jikelele angakaneli, ngaphandle kokubeka umthwalo ongalinganiko.

[/glossary-entry]

[glossary-entry]

### Ukusabalalisa kabutjha

Ukutjhuguluka ngamabomu kwendlela izinto eziphathekako, amathuba, iinzuzo nofana imithwalo esabalaliswa ngayo, kungaba ngaphakathi kwenhlangano nofana ngamalungiselelo wezenhlalakahle nomphakathi.

[/glossary-entry]

[glossary-entry]

### Umnqopho ohlanganyelweko

Umnqopho ofanako onikela ihlangano isizathu sokuba khona begodu iqondise ukusetjenziswa kwayo kwemithombo.

[/glossary-entry]

[glossary-entry]

### Ukuzibandakanya okusekelweko

Ukuzibandakanya lapho umuntu asebenzisa khona isizo, ukutolika, ukusekelwa kwezokuthintana, namkha enye isizo ngesikhathi agcina i-ejensi yakhe nokuma kwezepolotiki.

[/glossary-entry]

[glossary-entry]

### Umklamo wendawo yoke

Ukuklama iindawo, ilwazi, iinkambiso namathulusi ukwenzela bona asetjenziswe babantu abanengi kusukela ekuthomeni kunokobana kuthembele ekutjhugululweni komuntu ngamunye.

[/glossary-entry]

[glossary-entry]

### Ukuhlanganyela ngokuzithandela

Inhlangano eyakhiwe ngokuvumelana hlangana nabantu kunokobana ibe lilunga eliphoqelekileko nofana eyakhiwe ngaphambilini mbuso.

[/glossary-entry]

[/glossary]
