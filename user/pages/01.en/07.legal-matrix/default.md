# Legal matrix

A public, updateable research resource for the [Association Constitution Drafting Tool](/en/stadgar). It brings legal forms, registration and tax statuses, activity-specific regulation, and democratic design choices into a shared map.

**Initial research release — all records await verification.** These entries identify questions and research leads, not verified drafting instructions. A legal-status label records the basis being investigated; it does not establish that the entry accurately states current law or applies to a particular association. No initial record is ready for use by the generator.

## Reading the matrix

Legal status, provision type, and review status answer different questions. An enacted law may still require research into its current wording, commencement, and applicability. A proposed reform is tracked separately and must not be treated as an existing obligation.

- **Legal requirement:** a candidate duty to verify against the applicable legal source and circumstances.
- **Democratic choice:** a decision for members to make, within any applicable legal limits.
- **Prudent safeguard:** a protective practice to consider, without assuming it is legally required.
- **Institutional pattern:** one possible way to organise, rather than a mandatory model.

The initial coverage follows [the white paper’s legal mapping agenda](/en/white-paper#part-i-25). Form, registration status, tax status, and activities are recorded separately so that one label does not stand in for all the others. The resource is currently English-only.
